## 1b. How each lesson is sequenced (research: `docs/research/academy-lesson-sequencing.md`)
Teaching episodes use **PPP with gradual release**: context (the story) → **model** (I do) → **rule** (stated, 3 lines) → **controlled practice** (we do: games with one right answer) → **free practice** (choices) → **produce** (you do: Mission Take 1, feedback, Take 2, Release) → teach-back. Episodes where the learner already has the language use a task-first order: E3 Pattern Lab = test-teach-test / guided discovery; E4 and E6 and E8 = task-based (pre-task, task, language focus). A test checks the order model → rule → controlled → free → produce in every deck that sets `stage` on its slides.

## 3. Modelling and the rule in every lesson (owner, 2026-10-13)
Every lesson has: (a) a **teacher model** before the student tries anything new (I do, we do, you do), for the language and for the Mission task; (b) **the rule stated plainly** in at most 3 lines, with an example each, shown, read aloud and repeated by the student; (c) the student **restates it** at the end (teach-back). In this Season: E1 states "like + thing / like + -ing / love is stronger"; E2 the verb chunks (play + sport, go + -ing, do + activity); E3 the full rule (I / you / we / they like; do-support in questions and short answers; don't); E4 the question-answer pattern; E5 how to read a poster (day, time, place); E6 love / hate and "a lot / a bit"; E7 and E8 recall the rules.

## 3b. Teacher notes
- Likely errors and prompts: "I am like music" (prompt: "I … music?"), "I like play football" (clue: "I like football / I like playing football"), "I no like" (do-support is stated as a rule in E3; tonight just model "I like" and move on), silent -ing. Log them; fix only in the feedback step.
- Wait 5-7 seconds. Offer type-instead-of-speak. Never compare with other students.
- The rule is stated tonight (3 lines, after the models and before the practice): like + thing, like + -ing, love is stronger. Do not add he/she/it, don't/do-support (E3) or "to" + verb.
- Cultural check: pick hobbies the student could really do (no assumptions about sports clubs or pools); the poster pictures include music, games, drawing, cooking, football.
- Plan B (no tech): the story as 8 lines read aloud, hobby words on paper cards; same games with real cards.

## 4. Checks
- Sequencing: no word is asked before it is introduced; "I like" is heard before it is said.
- Variety: games alternate (memory, odd-one-out, point, hot seat, this-or-that, role-play); no kind twice in a row.
- Fun ingredients (5+): a mystery poster, a student badge, a role-play with Ava, a smudged clue, humour in Hot Seat.

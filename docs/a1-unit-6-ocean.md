# A1 Unit 6 · Ocean — unit plan and lesson plans

Same method as Units 1-5 (`docs/a1-unit-1-welcome-town.md` … `docs/a1-unit-5-monster-town.md`, all owner-approved): the
lesson blueprint (`docs/playground-lesson-blueprint.md` §3g game menu), the quality skills and fresh research
(`docs/research/playground-a1u6-research.md`). In A1 the child reads **full sentences**: **one story read a bit more each
lesson**, **phonics + a phonics game + Blend & Read + a tongue twister in every lesson**, **30-49 pages per lesson**, **no
game twice in the unit**, **no story videos** (Canva story GIFs only after a scenario and the owner's OK). Every activity
gets the **aesthetic pass** in the Playground colours (`gameSkin.ts`), and after new words always comes **Read and
Match**, then another game.

**Status:** approved (owner, 2026-10-15: "Go") — building.

## 1. Where we start

- Roadmap (`docs/a1-playground-roadmap.md`, Unit 6) and the seven lesson plans in `src/curriculum/roadmap/a1LessonPlans.ts`:
  sea animals, *what it is doing* (-ing), where it lives and what it eats, *Is it…?* questions, the story *The Treasure
  Reef*, a question game and the Boss Test. The world is **Ocean Explorers** in `src/curriculum/worlds/a1Worlds.ts`
  (an underwater reef, a submarine, coral and bubbles; **Coral the Mermaid** and **Bub the Octopus**; palette ocean
  blues — pictures still carry the ~35 % purple line, here as purple coral and sea fans).
- What the child already has: **A1 Unit 2** *It can swim / It's got a long tail* and **sh** (*ship, shell, fish*);
  **A1 Unit 3** weather and **-ing** as a sound; **A1 Unit 5** *He's wearing…* (the first *-ing* sentence), *Has he got…?*
  and Guess Who; **Pre-A1** *fish* and the action words *swim, run, jump, eat, sleep*. So Unit 6 revises those fast and
  spends the time on what is new: six sea animals, **is + -ing** for what an animal is doing now, **It lives in / It eats**,
  and asking **Is it…? / Is it + -ing?**
- What this plan changes from the roadmap: (1) the story runs through L1-L5 (A1 has no story videos); (2) **phonics**:
  the roadmap puts **sh** in L1, but sh was taught in Unit 2, so L1 takes **wh** (*whale*, the lesson's word), L2 takes
  **oa** (*boat, float* — new), L3 takes **sh at the end** (*fish, splash* — a new position), L4 reviews; (3) the L3
  listening game is never "who said it?" (blueprint rule).

## 2. Unit objective

**By the end of Unit 6 the child can name six sea animals, say what each one is doing and where it lives, find a hidden
animal by asking yes/no questions, and read *The Treasure Reef*.**

> *"It's a turtle. It's swimming. It lives in the sea and it eats sea grass." — "Is it a crab? Is it hiding?"*

Evidence at the end: the Boss Test (5 parts, 4 of 5 each) and a 3-question hunt the child leads.

## 3. The unit's language

| | New | Recycled |
|---|---|---|
| Sea animals | shark, whale, turtle, starfish, octopus, crab (+ shell, seahorse as extras) | fish, big / small, *It's got…*, *It can…* |
| Doing now | *What's it doing? It's swimming / hiding / diving / eating / sleeping / walking.* · plural *They're swimming.* | swim, eat, jump, sleep (Pre-A1); *He's wearing* (U5) |
| Where / what | *It lives in the sea / on the beach / on the reef. It eats fish / plants / shells.* | in / on |
| Asking | *Is it a…? Is it swimming? Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.* | *Has he got…?* (U5) |
| Story extras | sunny, windy, boat, treasure, wave | weather (U3) |
| Sight words | what, doing, lives, eats, isn't | is, it, the, a |
| Sounds | **wh** (whale, white, wheel, what) · **oa** (boat, float, coat, soap, goat) · **sh at the end** (fish, dish, wish, splash) · L4-L7 review | sh (U2), -ing (U3) |

All sounds come from our recorded sounds (`phon-wh`, `phon-o-long`, `phon-sh`), so no new recording is needed.

## 4. The reading strand — *The Treasure Reef* (12 pages, read a bit more each lesson)

The friends (Leo, Pip, Mia and Biscuit) follow the fifth piece of Willow's Golden Compass to the sea. **Coral the
Mermaid** (a girl) and **Bub the Octopus** (a shy boy who hides everywhere) show them the reef. The compass piece is in
a treasure chest, but the chest is lost in a storm. Every animal helps — the language is the plot: what each animal is
doing and where it lives.

| Page | Picture | Text (decodable + sight words) | Lesson |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | a sunny beach, a little boat | *It is sunny. Look! A boat on the sea.* | L1 |
| 2 | Coral waving from a rock | *This is Coral. She lives in the sea.* | L1 |
| 3 | a big whale and a turtle | *It is a whale. It is big! It is a turtle.* | L1 |
| 4 | Bub half behind a rock | *This is Bub. Bub is an octopus. He is hiding.* | L2 |
| 5 | a crab on the sand, a starfish | *The crab is walking. The starfish is sleeping.* | L2 |
| 6 | the friends diving with masks | *The friends are diving. Fish are swimming!* | L3 |
| 7 | the reef, turtle eating sea grass | *The turtle lives on the reef. It eats sea grass.* | L3 |
| 8 | the sky goes dark, big waves | *Oh no! It is windy. The waves are big.* | L4 |
| 9 | Mia asks the shark | *Mia: Is it the chest? Shark: No, it isn't!* | L4 |
| 10 | Bub points under a rock, chest glowing | *Bub is not hiding now. He is helping!* | L5 |
| 11 | the chest opens, the compass piece | *Is it the treasure? Yes, it is!* | L5 |
| 12 | everyone swimming in the sun | *It is sunny again. Bub is happy. Splash!* | L5 |

Pictures: Canva (owner rule), the Unit 1 cast + Biscuit + new cut-out stickers **Coral** (one per action: waving,
swimming, pointing, laughing), **Bub** (hiding, swimming, eating, pointing, happy, scared), **shark, whale, turtle,
starfish, crab, seahorse** each in 2 actions (swimming / eating / sleeping / hiding / walking as fits), shell, boat,
chest. Clothes rule (owner, 2026-10-15: "make sure the characters are covered"): everyone is modestly and fully covered — long-sleeved swim shirts (rash guards) and knee-length swim shorts at the beach, masks for diving; Coral wears a long-sleeved, high-neck teal swim shirt that meets her tail (no bare shoulders or tummy); boys never in a dress or skirt.
~35 % purple (purple coral, sea fans, a violet sunset), mixed with sea blues, teal, sand and green plants.

## 5. Lesson by lesson

**Every lesson** (same routine as Units 1-5): title → Remember? → model (meet / echo, **I do → we do → you do**) →
**Read and Match** → first game → (song after the first practice game, L3) → games → **Reader** (new story pages) →
reading game → **sound model → phonics game → Blend & Read → tongue twister** → free talk → check · sticker · **Pet Pal**
· home mission · goodbye song · finale. 30-49 pages; every page names its objective part in the teacher note
("Guided:" / "Free:").

### 6.1 Lesson 1 — Meet the Sea Animals: Shark, Whale

**Objective:** *I can name sea animals: "It's a whale. They're sharks."*
**Evidence:** the child catches and names 5 animals in full sentences.

| Part | Games |
|---|---|
| Revise (fast) | Remember? (Unit 5: feelings + looks) · *fish, swim, big, small* in one quick round |
| Model | one animal per page (shark, whale, turtle, starfish), then *They're…* with a group picture (one structure per page) · echo |
| Match | **Read and Match** (sentence ↔ animal) |
| Practice | **I Spy binoculars** (spy the animal Coral names) · **Deep-Sea Fishing** (signature: fish the animal you hear, say *It's a…* / *They're…*) · **Photo Snap** (underwater camera: *Take a photo of the turtles!*) · **Shadow Match** (animal outlines) |
| Reading | Reader p1-3 · reading game |
| Phonics | **wh** model · **Letter Moles (wh)** · Blend & Read: *when, whip, what* · twister *Why do white whales whistle?* |
| Talk | *What's your favourite sea animal? It's a…* + ask the teacher *What's yours?* |

### 6.2 Lesson 2 — More Sea Animals: Octopus, Crab

**Objective:** *I can say what a sea animal is doing: "The octopus is hiding. The crab is walking."*
**Evidence:** the child lights up 5 animals by saying what each one is doing.

| Part | Games |
|---|---|
| Model | octopus, crab; then one action per page: hiding, walking, diving, eating, sleeping, swimming (Bub acts it: *What's Bub doing? He's hiding.*) · echo |
| Match | **Read and Match** (*-ing* sentence ↔ picture) |
| Practice | **Live Reef ★ (new)** — a living reef in soft fog; the animals peek out; the child says what one is doing (*The crab is walking.*) and that part of the reef lights up and comes alive (approved silent loop or UI motion only); a wrong verb = the animal shrugs and does it again · **Catch & Sort** (swimming / hiding) · **Flip & Say** (flip a card, say the sentence) |
| Reading | Reader p4-5 · **Picture Maze** (follow the sentence path) |
| Phonics | **oa** model · **Word Splat (oa)** · Blend & Read: *boat, coat, goat* → *The boat can float.* · twister *A goat in a coat floats on a boat.* |

### 6.3 Lesson 3 — Listen: Where Do They Live?

**Objective:** *I can understand where animals live and what they eat: "It lives in the sea. It eats fish."*
**Evidence:** 4 of 5 right from listening.

| Part | Games |
|---|---|
| Model | *It lives in the sea / on the beach / on the reef.* — *It eats fish / plants / shells.* (one sentence per page) |
| Song | **"Down in the Deep Blue Sea"** (new, kids-song-writer: one verse per animal — what it is doing, where it lives; one take) after the first practice game |
| Practice | **Sort Basket** (signature: sea / beach / reef — the animal swims home when sorted right and the child says *It lives…*) · **Musical Islands** (listen, jump to the right island) · **Draw the Path** (listen: the turtle swims to the reef, past the crab…) · **Sticker Bingo** (hear the fact, cover the animal) |
| Reading | Reader p6-7 · reading game |
| Phonics | **sh at the end** (fish, dish, wish, splash) · **Letter Bubbles** (anagram: f-i-s-h) · Blend & Read · twister *Fish wish for a big splash.* |

### 6.4 Lesson 4 — Ask and Answer: Is It a Shark?

**Objective:** *I can find a hidden animal by asking: "Is it a crab? Is it swimming?" "Yes, it is. / No, it isn't."*
**Evidence:** the child finds the secret animal with 3-4 questions, then hides one and answers.

| Part | Games |
|---|---|
| Model | *Is it a…?* and *Is it swimming?* (one page each), short answers |
| Practice | **Secret Card** (signature: the child asks, clues pin to a **detective board** until the animal is found; then swap) · **Field Guide** (the child is the expert: Bub wrote the reef guide and got it wrong — *The shark is sleeping. No! It's swimming.*) · **Go Fish!** (*Have you got a crab?*) · **Treasure Islands** (find the chest: *Is it on the reef?*) |
| Reading | Reader p8-9 · reading game |
| Phonics | review wh / oa / sh · **Letter Blocks** · Blend & Read · twister of the unit |
| Talk | free mini-conversation with Coral: the child asks her 3 questions |

### 6.5 Lesson 5 — Storybook: The Treasure Reef

**Objective:** *I can read "The Treasure Reef" with help and say what the animals are doing and what the weather is like.*
**Evidence:** reads 4 pages alone; at the pause chooses who can help find the chest and says why; retells 3 pictures.
Predict → **Reader** (the whole book, a **pause page** after p9: which animal can help? *Bub! He's good at hiding — he
knows the rocks.*) → **Lift the Flap** (what's hiding where in the story) → **Tile Reveal** (which page is it?) → act it
out → phonics review (**Letter Monsters**: wh, oa, sh) → Blend & Read → twister → check · sticker · Pet Pal · home ·
goodbye · finale. Story GIFs (Canva) only after a written scenario, a preview GIF and the owner's OK.

### 6.6 Lesson 6 — Ocean Question Game! (extra practice)

**Objective:** *I can ask and answer questions about sea animals quickly and correctly.*
Games (different from L1-L5, accuracy before speed): **Buzzer Show** (game show, pearls for every right question) ·
**Memory** (new design; say the *-ing* sentence to keep a pair) · **Hot Coconut** (*Is it swimming?* before the coconut
passes) · **Tic-Tac-Toe Duel** · **Wobbly Tower** · **Team Flags** · Twister Race · Blend & Read · free talk with Bub.

### 6.7 Lesson 7 — Boss Test: Ocean Explorer!

| Part | Format | Pass |
|---|---|---|
| Warm-up | **Rising River** (co-op) | — |
| 1 Listening | what is it doing / where does it live → tap (5) | 4/5 |
| 2 Reading — yes / no | sentences about a big reef picture (5) | 4/5 |
| 3 Reading — the story | 5 gaps in *The Treasure Reef* | 4/5 |
| 4 Speaking | describe 2 animals (name, doing, lives) + find a hidden animal with questions (5 points) | 4/5 |
| 5 Reading aloud | one story page (5) | 4/5 |

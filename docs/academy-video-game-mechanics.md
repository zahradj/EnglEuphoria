# Academy video-game mechanics list (living list)

Owner rule 2026-10-14: every Academy unit (Season) and lesson names at least one mechanic borrowed from a video game, so students have a blast. Companion to `docs/academy-games-catalog.md` (148 classroom games). Mechanics only, never characters, content or music; teen-appropriate (13-18); recorded voices only; motion rules in CLAUDE.md apply.

**Status:** seed list from general game-design knowledge, **unverified**. The next research pass (units: see `docs/research/`) must confirm, extend and log sources per mechanic. Add rows as lessons use them.

| Mechanic | Video-game family | What the student does in English | Best for | Comfort |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Quest log + clue unlock | Adventure / RPG | Completes language tasks to unlock the next clue of the Season story | every Season | Low |
| Boss fight (rounds with a weak spot) | Action RPG | Answers rapid retrieval items; each correct one damages the boss; wrong = gentle retry | review, Remix | Med |
| Loot and crafting | Survival / crafting | Collects words, combines them into phrases/sentences to craft an item | vocabulary, collocations | Low |
| Escape room | Puzzle / point-and-click | Reads or hears clues to open locks | reading, listening | Low |
| Detective board | Mystery | Collects evidence sentences, connects names, times, places | reading, past tenses | Low |
| Rhythm lane | Rhythm | Taps stressed syllables or words in time with a beat | pronunciation, stress | Med |
| Shop / trading sim | Management sim | Buys, sells and negotiates with the guide | functional language | Low |
| Dialogue tree | Visual novel / RPG | Chooses replies; consequences show register and politeness | speaking, pragmatics | Low |
| Tower defence | Strategy | Places the correct word/form to stop an incoming error | grammar accuracy | Med |
| Speedrun vs own best | Platformer | Repeats a route of items against his or her own record (no leaderboards) | fluency, recall | Med |
| Skill tree | RPG progression | Sees can-do skills unlock and level up after each Season | motivation, portfolio | Low |
| Stealth (quiet path) | Stealth | Picks polite/indirect phrases to pass guards | politeness, modals | Low |

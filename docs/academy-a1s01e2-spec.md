## 1. Blueprint facts kept (A1-S01-E2, Word Lab; `buildLessonBlueprint`)

60 minutes: Check-in 5 · Remember? 7 · The Drop 10 · Notice & Build 10 · Energiser 4 · Mission 12 · Release 7 · Wrap 5. Cast Vee, Ava, Theo (no Mia).
Stack "L2 = L1 + new": the Mission and the Release reuse lesson 1 (name, age, country, "How old are you?"). 14 new core-productive items = the blueprint's own list:
**one … ten, Spain, Spanish, Italy, Italian**. Clue 2 of 8. Supports: full frames, L1 hint, 0.8x, speaking ceiling repeat / substitute / answer / ask.
The Season can-do "I can spell my name and read a short online profile" and lexical fields (personal information, countries and nationalities, numbers, the alphabet, classroom language) set the situation.

## 2. Objective (academy-lesson-craft)

**By the end I can sign up at the club desk: spell my name, say my country and nationality, say my member number, and ask someone to say it again.**
CEFR A1 can-do: gives personal details (name, age, nationality), spells their name, says numbers, asks for repetition. Practical: online accounts, club forms, a phone call, a hotel desk.

## 3. Success criteria (the Wrap tick-list)

1. I spelled my name: M-I-N-A.
2. I said my number: My number is four, two, seven.
3. I said my country and nationality: I am Spanish.
4. I said my name and age. (lesson 1 recycled)
5. I asked for help: Can you say it again?

## 4. Exit task (Release, private first, model hidden)

Fill the club sign-up card (age, country, nationality; name and its spelling come from earlier steps; the member number is given by the desk), then say or type:
"My name is Mina. M-I-N-A. I am 14. I am from Brazil. I am Brazilian. My number is four, two, seven. Can you say it again?" The model comes back only on "Show me".
Reach test: every frame in it is read, completed, built or said at least 3 times before the Release (tested). A nationality that is not Spanish/Italian is typed by the student with a country → nationality list shown on the card (Spain → Spanish, Italy → Italian, Brazil → Brazilian, Japan → Japanese, France → French, USA → American): the *pattern* is taught, the list is a support, not new vocabulary.

## 5. Language inventory

- Words with a picture + meaning + question + clue (14): one … ten (number cards drawn in code: the digit and that many dots), Spain / Italy (flags drawn in code), Spanish / Italian (a person holding the flag).
- Frames: My name is ___. · How do you spell it? · A-V-A. · How old are you? · I am ___. · Where are you from? · I am from ___. · I am ___ (nationality). · My number is ___. · Sorry, can you say it again?
- Glossed, not taught: sign up, desk, spell, number, member, nationality, goal.

## 6. Route and alignment

| Part (min) | Screens | Trains |
|---|---|---|
| Check-in (5) | Vee + goal · Remember? form (name, age with models and sentence starters) · Ava/Theo greet · cloze of lesson 1 | 4 |
| Learn the words (7) | 4 sets (1-4, 5-7, 8-10, countries/people): a full page per word with question + clue, then a drag-the-word-onto-its-picture page | 2, 3 |
| The Drop = story (10) | Full-page sign-up scene: Ava and Theo act 16 lines (read, then say each key line); 3 understanding checks + match question/answer | 1-5 |
| Notice & Build (10) | **NEW: spell it** (letter tiles onto a name badge: Ava, Theo, YOUR name) · say it · read numbers ×2 · build "My number is four, two, seven." · match country → nationality (flags) · notice the pattern · build · 2 cloze conversations · build "How do you spell it?" | 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 |
| Energiser (4) | decode letters (S-A-M), next letter, match digits and words, odd one out, spot the mistake, "what do you say?" | 1, 2, 3, 5 |
| Mission (12) | read-and-repeat fades (keys hidden → first word only) · Chill/Normal/Push · Take 1 role-play (name, spell, where from) · feedback · Take 2 (Theo is fast: ask again, say your number) | 1-5 |
| Release (7) | sign-up card form with model · card · exit record (model hidden) · Ava/Theo react | 1-5 |
| Wrap (5) | 5 can-do ticks · homework "spell your name to one friend" · clue 2 | 1-5 |

Tests: every interactive screen tagged; each criterion ≥ 3 practice screens, last = production; exit frames ≥ 3 practices; cold-exit run (card `Mina / M-I-N-A / 14 / Brazil / Brazilian / 4-2-7`); fading order; minutes per part; 95 % known words.

## 7. Mechanics (academy-activity-selector) and variety vs lesson 1

Vocabulary: word page with question + clue + drag-match (lesson 1 style). **New mechanic: Spell It (name badge, letter tiles, drag or tap)** — Spelling-Bee / Wordle-style letter play made for beginners (see research). New drawn pictures (number cards with dots, flags, flag-person) so meaning needs no translation. Different from lesson 1: scene (sign-up desk), story frame (registering a friend; a spelling clue to the fake profile), mechanic (spelling, numbers, passports).

## 8. Known gaps (not hidden)

No recorded voices: letters, numbers and the story are silent, so there is **no listening-to-letters/numbers activity yet**; spelling is copy-spelling and read-aloud (the live teacher hears it). This is the biggest gap of the lesson and the first thing voices will unlock (dictation of names and numbers). Owner approval and the Academy hand-off rule are pending. Real two-device live sync is untested.

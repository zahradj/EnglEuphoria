## 1 Student and objective
One teen (13-18) at B1, one-to-one, home language ar / es / fr / tr / it. From lesson 1 they know the bucket-list words (bucket list, dare, give it a go, once in a lifetime, tick it off, brave), the participles (flown, eaten, gone, taken, been, seen) and "Have you ever …? / I have never …", and mix "when" (past simple) with "up to now" (present perfect). **Need now:** words to say *how an experience felt* and *where and what they did*; today they say "it was good / nice / very beautiful" for everything.
**Hard for them (L1, from `l1Watch` B1-S01 and the research):** es / it / fr "embarrassed" is read as embarazada / imbarazzato; the -ed / -ing contrast ("I am boring" for "I am bored"); word stress (fr and it: embARRassed, disappoINTed); -ed endings read as a full syllable (tr).
**Objective (observable):** By the end I can use 14 new words and chunks to say how an experience felt and where I did what, in a one-minute voice note about my own experiences.
**Exit task (the Release):** a private 30-45 second voice note "my words": at least five of the 14 words, one "I have …" sentence about an experience (lesson 1) and one past-simple follow-up with a time; model hidden.
**Success criteria (teacher marks in the checklist, 1-5 "I can …"):**
1. I can say what each of the eight feeling words means and use it about myself ("I felt … when …").
2. I can use destination, attraction and countryside in a sentence about a trip.
3. I can use go camping, go sightseeing and try something new in a sentence (present perfect or past simple).
4. I can describe an experience so a partner finds the right postcard, using at least three new words.
5. I can record a voice note with at least five of the words, an "I have …" sentence and a past-simple follow-up.
**New items (14, core productive, from the Season bank):** excitement, amazed, disappointed, embarrassed, proud, frightened, relaxed, grateful (feelings) · destination, attraction, countryside (places) · go camping, go sightseeing, try something new (chunks). Known-word coverage of every text ≥ 95 % (lesson 1 words, recycled A2-S13 / A2-S14 words; the rest glossed). The -ing forms (amazing, exciting) are **passive gloss only**, not new items.

## 2 Look, story frame and variety (vs lesson 1)
Lesson 1 was the evening rooftop with the bucket-list wall (note: that look breaks the 2026-10-15 rule "bright daytime school, light"; its plates are still to be made and should follow the rule). **Lesson 2:** the **Travel Club room** in a bright daytime school: tall sunny windows, light wood, a big world map, a wall of postcards, soft lilac accents (about 25 % purple), modest school clothes. Frame: **Vee's Postcard Wall**: the class sends postcards from their bucket-list trips and Vee needs the right word for each moment. New mechanics vs lesson 1 (Hot Seat, Break the Clock, Participle Wordle, Rapport, Triple Match, Trophy Forge, Glitch Hunt, Verb Draw): Mood Postcards, Word Workbench (crafting), Word Connections, Cause or Feeling? sort, Collocation Builder, Feelings Wheel Spin, Info-Gap Postcards. Cast (blueprint): **Vee** sets the word challenge; **Ava** asks "what is this?" and "how do you say …?"; Theo and Mia appear on postcards.

## 4 Speaking (B1 target: student talk ≥ 55 %, ≥ 20 speaking minutes, ≥ 5 speaking tasks, reaches the *free* step)
Speaking minutes ≈ 25: wheel sentence 1, spoken recall 3, saying each word and every game answer aloud 7, Never Have I Ever 2, Mission Take 1 + Take 2 + feedback 8, Release 3, teach-back 1. Ladder: shadow (say after the guide) → repeat → controlled (game answers, Workbench cards) → guided (Mission frames, Chill) → free (Normal / Push, Release). Voice notes between lessons: 3 a week (Daily 10).

## 5 Practice ladder, interleaving and the error loop
Guided (teach pages, games) → semi-independent (Workbench, sort) → independent (Mission Normal) → mixed (Never Have I Ever mixes the lesson-1 participles with the new words). Old and new are interleaved in Remember? and the Energiser. **Error loop:** misses in the games re-appear in the next set; the three logged errors return in the next Remember? and Daily 10.

## 6 Games, video-game mechanic and the signature game
From the **171-game catalogue**: 97 Bucket-list Recall (Flip & Recall), 4 Word Connections, 10 Collocation Builder, 60 Information Gap, 169 Never Have I Ever, 1 Taboo-lite (Push dial), plus the vocabulary-menu games drag-match, listen-pick, shadow-match, category sort. **New finds:** Mood Postcards (Dixit-style), Cause or Feeling? sort, Feelings Wheel Spin. **Video-game mechanic: Loot and crafting** (Word Workbench): the student collects a feeling, a reason and a place, and crafts a sentence card; what they say in English: a full sentence about their own experience (the Season trophy case from lesson 1 keeps the crafted cards). **Signature game:** Mood Postcards. Sources, rejected candidates and limits (the crafting grid is our own design, effect untested): research file §2. No game is repeated from lesson 1; no three of one kind in a row.

## 9 Supports, assessment and follow-up
Supports: home-language hint on request; slower audio; Chill track; Stretch (Push dial). Comfort: private before public, no timers on speaking, type-instead-of-speak always, no ranking, "Not yet – try again". Assessment: the five criteria in the teacher checklist; production-weighted evidence from the Mission and Release; delayed check in the next Remember?. Follow-up: Daily 10 on days 1, 3, 7, 14, 30; voice notes; the next clue unlocks "using three new words in sentences of your own".
**Teacher notes:** wait 5-7 s; correct after the Mission; log up to 3 errors; listen for "embarazada / imbarazzato" for *embarrassed*, "I am boring", stress on the wrong syllable, -ed as a full syllable.

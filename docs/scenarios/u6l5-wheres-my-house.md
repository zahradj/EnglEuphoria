## Beats
1. **HOOK** — Pip is playing in the park at sunset: "It's late! Time to go home!" … on the street: "Oh no! Where's my house?"
2. **MODEL** — Pip remembers his house ("My house has a red door"), then tries two wrong houses: a blue door (No!), a yellow door with a cat (No!).
3. **CHILD'S TURN** — at each door Pip asks "Is this my house?" → 3 s pause (the child answers) → Pip answers.
4. **PAYOFF** — the red door opens, Mom: "Welcome home, Pip!" — hug — Pip in his own bedroom: "I love my house!" → next page: the door-knocking game.

## How it moves (no paid video)
Each moment has TWO pictures of the same scene, before and after (Pip's paw up → knocking; door shut → door open with Mom).
The film flips between them in time with the line, like a flip-book (same method as U5L5). Action words light up
karaoke-style ("Knock, knock!"). No zoom, no pan; stills hold still and cross-fade between shots.

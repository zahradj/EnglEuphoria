# Scenario: A2 Season 1, Episode 1 "The Photo Wall" (story plates)

Learning goal: understand a short story (Ava has 20 photos and cannot remember last weekend) and hear was / were / -ed in use, then guess where Ava was. Format: four full scene plates (16:9, Canva, cast painted in, about 35 % purple), recorded cast voices, subtitles, cross-fade only, no zoom or pan. No video, no GIF.

| Plate | Picture | Lines (one per tap) | Calm space |
|---|---|---|---|
| story-wk-1 | Vee, Ava, Theo at the gallery wall of pinned photos, golden hour | Vee: "Welcome to the Photo Gallery!" · Ava: "Look at my photos from last weekend." | bottom fifth |
| story-wk-2 | Ava holds up her phone with a lake photo, puzzled; Theo leans in | Ava: "But I do not remember last weekend!" · Theo: "Not one thing? Look, a lake!" | top third |
| story-wk-3 | Theo points at three big photos (tent by a lake, wedding crowd, gift box); Ava beside him | Theo: "A tent, a crowd, a wedding. Where were you?" | bottom fifth |
| story-wk-4 (wrap clue) | Ava's hand holds a torn half-photo: a smiling person in a purple hoodie by a lake, red arrow on the wall | Ava: "Half a photo! Someone in a purple hoodie was at the lake. Who is it?" | bottom third |

Prediction pause: after plate 3 the student walks the photo wall (Explore) instead of answering a question. Place and event words are drawn as nine stickers (one sheet): lake, hill, camp, crowd, fishing, wedding, celebrate, surprise, joke. Plus one background plate `weekend-gallery`. Cost: six Canva generations, no video.

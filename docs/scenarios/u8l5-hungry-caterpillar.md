## Beats
1. **HOOK** — a tiny caterpillar pops out of a leaf: "I am hungry!"
2. **MODEL** — it visits three places of the unit and eats one food at each: "I like apples! / bread! / cake!" (one place per shot, the food word as a label).
3. **CHILD'S TURN (pause)** — "What does the caterpillar like?" — 4 s of silence on the three bitten foods — then the answer: "Apples, bread and cake!"
4. **PAYOFF** — it is full, sleeps in a cocoon, and wakes up as a butterfly; Pip waves: "Hello, butterfly!"

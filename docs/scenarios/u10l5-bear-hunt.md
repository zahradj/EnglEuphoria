## Beats
1. **HOOK** — "They have a map. They want to find a bear!"
2. **MODEL** — four places; at each, an animal shows an action ("It can jump!") and a friend copies it ("He can jump too!").
3. **CHILD'S TURN (pause)** — at the cave: "Look at the bear. What can it do?" — 4 s of silence on the sleeping bear — "It can sleep!"
4. **PAYOFF** — the bear wakes and dances; "It can dance! They can dance too! Hooray!"

## Beats
1. **HOOK** — "Let's find a bear!" The friends set off with a map.
2. **MODEL** — four places, four actions: tall grass (walk), river stones (jump), little river (swim), hill (run).
3. **CHILD'S TURN (pause)** — at the cave: "Shh! What is the bear doing?" — 4 s of silence on the sleeping bear — then "Sleeping!"
4. **PAYOFF** — the bear wakes, smiles; everybody claps and dances. "Hello, Bruno!"

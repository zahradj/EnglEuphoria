# Scenario: B1 Season 1, lesson 1 "Have You Ever...? · Cold Open" (story plates and the text)

Status: **approved by the owner on 2026-10-15 ("go"); plates and voices in progress.** Nothing below is made (no picture, no recording) until it is approved. The lesson plan (approved 2026-10-15, "go") is `docs/academy-b1-s01-spec.md`; research `docs/research/academy-b1-s01-research.md`.

Learning goal: understand, by listening and then reading, the main idea of a short exchange about bucket lists (Vee's dare, Ava's and Theo's experiences), meet six new words and six past participles in use, and hear "Have you ever eaten …?" in context, without being asked to produce it yet.

Format: **three full scene plates** (16:9, Canva, cast painted in from the approved references, about 25 % purple mixed with blue/teal, warm cream and wood tones, green plants, **no text or letters in the pictures**), spoken by the recorded cast voices with subtitles, cross-fade only, never zoom or pan. Teens 13–18: nothing childish.

Place (main plate of the lesson): **the rooftop studio with the bucket-list wall**: a big cork-and-glass wall of blank cards and polaroids in the evening light, a skyline behind, string lights. New place (not the club lobby, the fair or the vault).

## The text (about 190 words; written fresh, American English)

| # | Who | Line |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vee | Okay, listen. I have a dare for everyone. We all make a bucket list this term, and we tick it off before the break. |
| 2 | Vee | A bucket list is a list of things you want to do once in a lifetime. |
| 3 | Vee | Mine is ready. Number one: give surfing a go. I have never been in the ocean, but I have seen a hundred videos. |
| 4 | Ava | That is brave, Vee. I have been to the beach, but I have never tried surfing. |
| 5 | Ava | Last summer I went to Spain with my cousin. We ate pizza every day, and we took hundreds of photos. |
| 6 | Theo | Ava, you have flown across the world twice, and you say you are not brave? |
| 7 | Ava | Flying is easy, Theo. Falling off a surfboard is the scary part. |
| 8 | Theo | I have taken a photo in every country I have visited. That is my record. |
| 9 | Vee | So here is the dare: three things you have done, and three things you want to do. Who has gone the furthest? Who has eaten the strangest thing? |
| 10 | Ava | Wait. Have you ever eaten spicy noodles? |
| 11 | Vee | Ha! That is number two on my list. |

New receptive items met in the text (6 words and collocations): **bucket list · dare · give it a go · once in a lifetime · tick it off · brave**. New past participles met (6): **been · gone · seen · eaten · taken · flown**. Past simple follow-ups in the same text: went, ate, took. Known-word share (target at least 95 %) is checked with the item banks when the text is built; words flagged now: *surfing, ocean, cousin, skyline* (picture-supported). The gist question (clue 1 of 8): **"What is Vee's dare, and who sounds the bravest?"**

## Beats
1. **HOOK** — Pip and Dad at the park gate: "Today is my day with Dad! Just me and my dad!"
2. **MODEL** — four activities, one at a time, each said with "We…": play ball → feed the ducks → eat ice cream → read a book at home.
3. **CHILD'S TURN** — Pip asks "What do we do?" over a picture → 3 s pause (the child answers) → Pip answers.
4. **PAYOFF** — bedtime hug: "I love you, Dad!" → next page: the story game.

## How it moves (no paid video)
Each activity has TWO pictures of the same scene — before and after (Dad's arm back → the ball in the air; the bread in
Pip's paw → the bread on the water). The film flips between them in time with the line, like a cartoon flip-book (same
method the owner saw in Lesson 4/5). The action words light up karaoke-style as Pip says them ("Throw, catch!").
No zoom, no pan; stills hold still and cross-fade between shots.

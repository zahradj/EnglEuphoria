# Scenario: "The Postcard Wall" (B1-S01-E2 Word Lab) — three story plates

Status: written 2026-10-16 with the approved lesson plan (`docs/academy-b1-s01-e2-plan.md`, owner "go" 2026-10-16). Follows `.claude/skills/kids-video-scenario` in spirit (one learning goal, beats, shot list, pictures needed, cost) for **still plates only: no video, no AI clip, cross-fade only, never zoom or pan.** Picture generation (Canva, or Gemini via `bake-art.yml`) starts only after the owner approves the text in writing.

**One learning goal:** hear the new feeling, place and activity words in use before they are taught, so the student can predict and then recognise them (criteria 1 and 2).

**Look (CLAUDE.md, Academy pictures rule):** bright, light, airy, broad daylight, at school, age-appropriate, reserved; big sunny windows, light wood, a world map, a wall of postcards, soft lilac accents (about 25 % purple) mixed with blue-teal, warm cream and green plants; modest school clothes; calm actions; no night, dusk, neon or gloom.

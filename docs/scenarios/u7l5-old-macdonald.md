## Beats
1. **HOOK** — Pip at the farm gate at sunrise: "This is Grandpa's farm! Good morning, Grandpa!"
2. **MODEL** — in front of the barn with four closed doors: "The animals are in the barn. Listen!" — the first sound comes from door 1, Pip asks "What animal is this?", the door opens: "It's a cow!"
3. **CHILD'S TURN** — doors 2-4: a sound, Pip asks "What animal is this?" → **3 s pause** (the child answers) → the door opens and Pip says the answer.
4. **PAYOFF** — all four animals come out into the yard with Grandpa and Pip: "Grandpa had a farm, E-I-E-I-O!" → next page: the games.

## How it moves (no paid video)
Each moment has TWO pictures of the same scene (door shut → door open with the animal looking out). The film flips between
them in time with the line (same method as U6L5). Karaoke words light up ("Moo! Moo!"). No zoom, no pan; stills hold still and
cross-fade between shots. The barn pictures are edits of ONE base picture, so only the door that the line names changes.

# Scenario — U2L2 "The Magic Mix" (page 7, before the Magic Paint Pots game)

Skill: `.claude/skills/kids-video-scenario`. Status: **approved** 2026-10-04 — stills film in the lesson; motion clips one at a time.

- **Lesson:** Pre-A1 Unit 2 Lesson 2 "Green, Orange, Purple!" — page 7 (it teaches; page 8, Magic Paint Pots, lets the child do it).
- **Goal (one):** the child can say which two colours make green, orange and purple, and name the new colour.
- **Target words:** blue, yellow, red → green, orange, purple; "What color is it?" — "It's green!"
- **Setting / camera:** Pip's paint studio; one table, one glass bowl, two jars. ONE friend per part, full body,
  front view at eye level, the same camera for the whole film. Silent clips; the friends' recorded voices are added.
- **Length:** about 65 s (3 parts × ~20 s + ending).

## Beats
HOOK — "Pip needs green, orange and purple. Let's mix!" → MODEL — each friend pours two paints and stirs, step by step →
CHILD'S TURN — "What color is it?" + pause → PAYOFF — "It's green! Blue and yellow make green!" → "Now YOU mix!" (game).

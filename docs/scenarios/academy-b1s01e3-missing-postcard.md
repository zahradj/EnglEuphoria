# Scenario: "The Missing Postcard" (B1-S01-E3 Pattern Lab): three story plates and the case-board look

Status: written 2026-10-16 with the approved lesson plan (`docs/academy-b1-s01-e3-plan.md`; owner "go, start creating the lesson" 2026-10-16). Still plates only: no video, no AI clip, cross-fade only, never zoom or pan. Cast per the blueprint: **Vee and Theo** (the plan's Ava / Mia statements were cut to keep to the blueprint's cast).

**One learning goal:** hear the present perfect for experience ("I have never been to Lisbon. I have been to Rome twice.") next to the past simple for details, before the rule, so the student can notice the difference (criteria 2 and 4).

**Look (CLAUDE.md, Academy pictures rule):** bright, light, airy, broad daylight, at school, age-appropriate, reserved; the school **library reading room**: tall sunny windows, light wood shelves, a long study table, a pinboard case board with index cards and red string, a globe; soft lilac cushions and book spines (about 25 % purple) mixed with blue-teal, warm cream and green plants; modest school clothes; calm, reserved actions. No night, dusk, neon, gloom, no crime scene or police tape: a puzzle, not a crime.

## Beats
1. **HOOK** — Pip shows his brand-new white shoes: "My shoes are white!"
2. **MODEL** — three steps, three colours: apples (red), paint (blue), mud (brown). Each time: "Oh no!" → the shoes change → "My shoes are red! I like my red shoes!"
3. **CHILD'S TURN (pause)** — "What colour are Pip's shoes now?" — 4 s of silence on Pip's brown shoes — then the answer: "Brown!"
4. **PAYOFF** — splash in a rain puddle, the shoes are white again; Pip and Mia: "I like my shoes!"

## Unit objective

**By the end of Unit 2 the child can describe a jungle animal in 4 short sentences — what it is, what it looks like, what it
can do and what it can't do — and ask and answer "Can it…?"**

> *"It's an elephant. It's big. It can swim. It can't climb."* — *"Can it fly?" "No, it can't."*

Evidence: the Boss Test (Lesson 7) — listening (words, sentences, sounds) + the child describing two animals and asking a
friend one "Can it…?" question; 80 % in every part.

### What the child brings from Pre-A1 (required knowledge)

- Animal words: dog, cat, bird, cow, pig, sheep, horse, duck, chicken, fish (Pre-A1 Unit 7); lion, bear, frog.
- Action words + *can*: run, jump, walk, swim, dance, sing, clap, fly; *I can… / I can't…*, *he/she/it can* (Pre-A1 Unit 10).
- Size: big / small (Pre-A1 Unit 4); body: head, eyes, legs, arms (Pre-A1 Unit 4).
- A1 Unit 1: *He/She is…*, *This is…*, *Is it a…?* (recognition).

So A1 Unit 2 is a **spiral step up**, not a repeat: new jungle animals, new verbs (climb, swing), *can't* and **questions**
with *it*, and joining sentences into a short description.

### Lesson 2 in detail (the next one to build)

- **Objective:** I can say what a jungle animal can and can't do — *"A monkey can climb. It can't fly."*
- **Needs it meets:** Pre-A1 taught *can* for people; A1 moves to *it* + **can't** and to two-sentence answers (Cambridge
  "give very basic descriptions"). Children of 6-9 learn actions best by doing them (TPR mime), so every action is shown
  and copied before it is used in a game.
- **New (≈ 25 %):** climb, swing, *It can't…*. **Re-used:** lion, monkey, bird, fish; run, jump, swim, fly; *It can…*.
- **Outcome at the end:** the child can look at any of the 4 animals and say one *can* and one *can't* sentence without help,
  and understand them when heard (listening game ≥ 4 of 5).
- **Phonics:** *ch* (chimp, chick, lunch) + Blend It!
- **Order (blueprint):** title → Remember? (Lesson 1 animals) → intro → action words (watch & copy, one action per page) →
  *can / can't* model → first game → **song** → Jungle Obstacle Run → speaking pages → phonics → review → sticker → home
  mission (*"Show a grown-up an animal: say what it can and can't do."*).

## Real-life transfer (every lesson's home mission)

L1 point to an animal picture and name it · L2 mime an animal for your family and say what it can and can't do · L3 sing the
song and ask a grown-up "Can it…?" · L4 describe your favourite animal in 3 sentences · L5 tell the story with the pictures ·
L6 play the animal game with a sibling · L7 show your medal and describe an animal.

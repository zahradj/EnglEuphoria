# A1 Unit 5 · Monster Town — unit plan and lesson plans

Same method as Units 1-4 (`docs/a1-unit-1-welcome-town.md` … `docs/a1-unit-4-food.md`, all owner-approved): the lesson
blueprint (`docs/playground-lesson-blueprint.md` §3g game menu), the quality skills and fresh research. In A1 the child
reads **full sentences**: **one story read a bit more each lesson**, **phonics + a phonics game + Blend & Read + a tongue
twister in every lesson**, **30-49 pages per lesson**, **no game twice in the unit**, **no story videos** (Canva story
GIFs only after a scenario and the owner's OK). Every activity gets the **aesthetic pass** (CLAUDE.md, 2026-10-14), and
after new words always comes **Read and Match**, then another game.

## 1. Where we start

- Roadmap (`docs/a1-playground-roadmap.md`, Unit 5) and the seven lesson plans in `src/curriculum/roadmap/a1LessonPlans.ts`
  exist: feelings + *because*, looks and family (*He's got / She's got*), the feelings song, *He's wearing…*, the story
  *Bibi's Bad Day*, Charades + Guess Who, the Boss Test and the **A1 Halfway Check**. The world is **Monster Academy**
  in `src/curriculum/worlds/a1Worlds.ts` (a magical classroom: floating books, potions, a starry ceiling; Professor Glob
  and Bibi the Tiny Monster; palette purple / indigo / pink / sky blue — it fits the ~35 % purple rule).

  you?*; **Pre-A1 Unit 4** taught body parts and Bo the monster (*Bo has three eyes, big feet*); **Pre-A1 Unit 9**
  taught clothes (*shirt, hat, shoes, socks, dress*) and *What are you wearing?*; **Pre-A1 Unit 5** taught family.

  and family, and *wearing* in the third person.

  every lesson; (3) **L1's title** becomes *How Do You Feel? Scared, Bored, Excited!* — the roadmap title names happy,

## 2. Unit objective

**By the end of Unit 5 the child can say how someone feels and why, describe what a monster (or a person) looks like,
who is in their family and what they are wearing, and read *Bibi's Bad Day*.**

> *"Bibi is scared because it is dark. She's got three eyes and long horns. She's got a little sister. She's wearing a
> big pink scarf. Now she's excited because it's her party!"*

Evidence at the end: the Boss Test (5 parts, 4 of 5 each), a 4-sentence monster portrait the teacher can draw, and the
**A1 Halfway Check** report for parents.

## 3. The unit's language

| | New | Recycled |
|---|---|---|
| Feelings | scared, excited, bored, surprised | happy, sad, tired, angry, hungry |
| Why | *because…* (chunk: *She's scared because it's dark.*) · *Why are you sad?* | *How are you?* |
| Looks | teeth, horn, tall, short (people), long / short hair; *He's got / She's got…* | eyes, ears, nose, big, small, long (U2 *It's got*) |
| Family | *She's got a little brother / sister.* | mom, dad, brother, sister, grandma (Pre-A1) |
| Clothes | wear, wearing, scarf, glasses, boots; *He's wearing…* | hat, shoes, socks, shirt, coat |
| Sight words | because, why, got, wear, she, her | is, has, a, the |
| Sounds | **ee** (see, bee, feet, green, three) · **ea** (eat, tea, sea, leaf, scream) · **ee / ea sort** · **y** at the end (happy, funny, sleepy) · L5-L7 review | gr, pl, sn, fr (Unit 4) |

All sounds are blended from our recorded single sounds (`public/lep1/audio/letters/phon-e-long.ogg` for ee / ea / y),
so no new recording is needed.

## 4. The reading strand — *Bibi's Bad Day* (12 pages, read a bit more each lesson)

The friends (Leo, Pip, Mia and Biscuit) follow the second piece of Willow's Golden Compass to **Monster Town**. At
**Monster Academy** they meet **Bibi the Tiny Monster** (a girl) and **Professor Glob**. Bibi's day goes wrong — it's
dark, the lesson is long, the big monsters are so tall — and she feels smaller and smaller. The friends ask *Why are
you sad?*, and plan a surprise. The language is the plot: feelings + *because*, *has got*, *wearing*, *Why…?*

| Page | Picture | Text (decodable + sight words) | Lesson |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Monster Town at sunrise: purple houses, the Academy | *This is Monster Town. Look! A tiny monster.* | L1 |
| 2 | Bibi yawning in bed | *This is Bibi. Bibi is tired because it is early.* | L1 |
| 3 | Bibi in a dark Academy hall | *It is dark. Bibi is scared!* | L1 |
| 4 | Professor Glob's long lesson | *The lesson is long. Bibi is bored.* | L2 |
| 5 | big monsters next to tiny Bibi | *Big Bob has got three eyes. He is very tall.* | L2 |
| 6 | Bibi and her little sister Bea | *Bibi has got a little sister. Her name is Bea.* | L3 |
| 7 | Bibi alone at lunch | *Bibi is sad because she is tiny.* | L3 |
| 8 | Leo, Pip and Mia arrive | *Leo is wearing a green scarf. Mia is wearing glasses.* | L4 |
| 9 | Mia asks | *Mia: Why are you sad, Bibi? Bibi: I am sad because I am little.* | L4 |
| 10 | the friends open a door: balloons | *Surprise! Bibi is surprised.* | L5 |
| 11 | Bibi jumping | *Bibi is excited because it is a party for her!* | L5 |
| 12 | everyone dancing, the compass piece glows | *Bibi is happy. It is a good day after all!* | L5 |

Pictures: Canva (owner rule), the Unit 1 cast + Biscuit + new cut-out stickers **Bibi the Tiny Monster** (a girl: one
sticker per feeling — happy, sad, tired, scared, bored, surprised, excited, angry), **Professor Glob**, **Big Bob**,
**Bea**. Clothes rule: Leo and Pip wear scarves, hats, boots and glasses — never a dress or skirt. ~35 % purple, mixed
with teal, cream and wood (owner colour rule).

## 5. Lesson by lesson

**Every lesson** (same routine as Units 1-4): title → Remember? → model (meet / echo, **I do → we do → you do**) →
**Read and Match** → first game → (song after the first practice game) → games → **Reader** (new story pages) →
reading game → **sound model → phonics game → Blend & Read → tongue twister** → free talk → check · sticker · **Pet Pal**
· home mission · goodbye song · finale. 30-49 pages; every page names its objective part in the teacher note.

### 5.1 Lesson 1 — How Do You Feel? Scared, Bored, Excited!

**Objective:** *I can say how someone feels and why: "She's scared because it's dark."*
**Evidence:** the child says 3 feeling sentences about the monsters, 2 with *because*.

| Part | Games |
|---|---|
| Revise (fast) | Remember? (Unit 4 foods) · old feelings in one quick round: **Feelings Flash** (rapid recall: happy, sad, tired, angry, hungry) |
| Model | the 4 new feelings one at a time on Bibi (meet, *look: word*, one word at a time: the face → *scared*), then **because** in two halves: *She's scared* → *because it's dark* (picture of the dark hall) · echo |
| Match | **Read and Match** (feeling sentences ↔ Bibi stickers) |
| Practice | **Monster Mood Machine ★ (new)** — a bubbling potion machine: pull the lever, out comes a reason picture (a dark hall, a party, a long lesson, a spider…); the child picks the feeling that fits and SAYS the whole sentence; the monster's face changes as they say it · **Which Monster?** (listen-tap: *Who is bored?* tap the monster) · **Yes or No?** (true-false on Monster Town pictures) |
| Reading | Reader p1-3 · reading game |
| Phonics | **ee** model · **Sound Fish (ee)** · Blend & Read (Train): *see, bee, feet* · twister *Three green bees see sheep.* |
| Talk | *How do you feel today? Why?* |

### 5.2 Lesson 2 — What Do They Look Like? Tall, Short, Big, Small

**Objective:** *I can describe looks and family: "She's got long hair. She's got a little brother."*
**Evidence:** the child builds 3 monster faces from listening and describes one in 2 sentences.

| Part | Games |
|---|---|
| Model | *He's got / She's got* + teeth, horns, eyes, hair; tall / short; family on Bibi's family photo |
| Match | **Read and Match** (looks sentences ↔ monster stickers) |
| Practice | **Face Builder** (build what you hear: *She's got three eyes and long horns.*) · **Monster Family Tree** (family-tree: *She's got a little sister.*) · **Size Line** (tall ↔ short monsters) |
| Reading | Reader p4-5 · **Read & Drag** |
| Phonics | **ea** model · **Word Build (ea)** · Blend & Read (Slide): *eat, sea, tea* · twister *Sea monsters eat peas at the beach.* |

### 5.3 Lesson 3 — Listen to the Feelings Song

**Objective:** *I can understand feelings and descriptions in a song and in short dialogues.*
**Evidence:** Listen & Tap 4 of 5 ("Name That Feeling").

| Part | Games |
|---|---|
| Song | **"How Do You Feel?"** (new, kids-song-writer: one verse per feeling, *because* line, a face to make in each chorus) after the first practice game |
| Practice | **Name That Feeling** (listen-tap game show) · **Tick or Cross** (who feels what and why) · **Echo Cave** (say it like the monster: scared / excited voice) |
| Reading | Reader p6-7 · **Read & Check** |
| Phonics | **ee / ea Sort** (sound-sort: same sound, two spellings — the child sorts by how it is WRITTEN) · Blend & Read (Gumball) · twister *Bea eats green peas.* |

### 5.4 Lesson 4 — Describe a Monster Friend!

**Objective:** *I can describe a monster in 4 sentences: "He's got three eyes. He's wearing a big hat. He's happy because…"*
**Evidence:** a 4-sentence description the teacher can build.

| Part | Games |
|---|---|
| Model | *He's wearing / She's wearing* + scarf, glasses, boots, hat (dress-up on Big Bob and Bibi; boys never in a dress) |
| Practice | **Monster Maker** (the child describes, the teacher builds it **wrong on purpose**, the child corrects it; then swap) · **Dress Studio** (dress the monster as you hear) · **Washing Line** (*Where is Bob's scarf?*) |
| Reading | Reader p8-9 · **Sentence Build** |
| Phonics | **y = /ee/** at the end (happy, funny, sleepy) · **Brick Crush (y)** · Blend & Read (Frog) · twister *Funny Bibi is happy and sleepy.* |

### 5.5 Lesson 5 — Storybook: Bibi's Bad Day

**Objective:** *I can read "Bibi's Bad Day" with help and say how Bibi feels and why.*
**Evidence:** reads 4 pages alone; at the pause chooses how to cheer Bibi up and says why (*She's sad because she's tiny.*).
Predict → **Reader** (the whole book, a **pause page** before p10: the child chooses how to cheer Bibi up) → **Feelings
Meter** (story-order of Bibi's feelings through the day) → **Story Order** → **Picture Crossword** (story words) → act it
out → phonics review (**Sound Pick**: ee, ea, y) → Blend & Read → twister of the unit → check · sticker · Pet Pal · home
· goodbye · finale. Story GIFs (Canva) only after a written scenario, a preview GIF and the owner's OK.

### 5.6 Lesson 6 — Feelings Charades! (extra practice)

**Objective:** *I can ask and answer about looks, clothes and feelings quickly.*
Games (different from L1-L5): **Guess Who ★ (new)** — a line-up of 8 monsters behind little doors; the child ASKS
*Has he got horns? Is he wearing glasses? Is he scared?*, the doors that don't fit flip down by themselves; then the
child hides a monster and answers the teacher's questions · **Charades** (mime a feeling, the partner says it AND why) ·
**Memory** · **Save the Balloon** (spell *scarf, teeth, sleep*) · **Treasure Chests** · Twister Race · Blend & Read · free
talk with Bibi.

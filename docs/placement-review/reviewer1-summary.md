## Overall assessment

Skill balance (author labels): grammar 42, vocabulary 13, listening 9, reading 9, writing 3, speaking 3.
- "Writing" and "speaking" items (A27-A32) are not writing or speaking: they are written error-recognition items. They cannot be reported as skill scores. Either relabel as grammar/pragmatics or remove the skill labels.
- Listening and reading are only 9 items each (23% of the bank combined). The only A1 reading items (A36, A47) are the same text type; no sign/menu/timetable/message type. No B1 listening beyond one; only two C1 receptive items each.
- Grammar is over-sampled (53%) and several structures repeat: be-verb at A1 (A01, A04, A41), past simple + yesterday (A05, A27, A49, A53), present perfect since/for (A09, A60), "so ... that" (A19, A26, A61), inversion (A20, A77, A79), "am liking" trap (A08, A30), relative pronouns (A63, A71).
- Under-represented: articles (a/an/the - the biggest L1 error area for Arabic/Turkish), countable/uncountable beyond A56, prepositions of time (in/on/at), quantifiers, present perfect experience (ever/never/yet), reported speech, modal deduction, word formation, collocation at B2. Only one item tests word order. No real item tests spelling or sentence-level discourse.
- Topic redundancy: weather (A06, A34, A51, A59, A67), movies (A05, A17, A27, A53, A55, A61, A72), phones in school (A38, A75), "family/introduce yourself" (A36, A47), film/book disappointing ending (A17, A76).
- Key position is badly skewed: option B is the key in 46 of 79 items (58%), A in 26, C in 7, D in 0. A test-wise student who never picks D and prefers B gains a lot. Shuffle options at delivery and make sure no fixed position pattern survives (my replacement items put the key in position A for convenience; shuffle them).
- Level calibration: A1 items are mostly good floor items; B2 items include several that are B1 or A2 (A14, A15, A18, A19). The C1 band has one cultural idiom (A21) and one that is B2 with a clue (A78). The C1 vocabulary items lean on negative distractors (A26).
- Americanisms: Grey (A33), cinema (A55), match (A67), film (A12), Hiya (A28) - fix; the bank otherwise uses AmE (soccer, movie, color, skepticism).
- Fairness: cognate-heavy vocabulary (enormous, meticulous, skepticism, maintain, compelling) favors French/Spanish/Italian speakers and is a known bias for Arabic and Turkish learners; high-value L1 traps (have 13 years, make attention, since + present) are good but should be balanced. No sensitive-topic problems; a Friday school trip (A54) is minor for Gulf learners. "Revert back" (A32) is a dialect issue and is fixed.

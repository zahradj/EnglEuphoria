# Rater 2: Academy placement bank review (79 MC items)

Verdicts: KEEP 33, FIX 33, REPLACE 13. Full per-item data is in rater2.json. Level judgments are my own professional ones. I did not have EGP or EVP access, and I flag the uncertain ones (for example "meticulous" B2 vs C1).

## Overall assessment

The bank is usable at A1-B1 grammar, but it is weak as a placement instrument for several reasons.

1. **Answer-position leak.** The key is in position B for 46 of 79 items (58%), A for 26, C for 7 and D for none. A test-wise student who always picks B scores about 58%. This is the biggest problem and the cheapest to fix: shuffle options at render time. Do not shuffle ordered options (A55 times, A46 weekdays).
2. **Difficulty numbers are unreliable.** Many items are tagged one level too high or low.
   - Tagged too high: A13 begin/start (B1 to A2), A16 look up (B2 to A2), A18 so (B2 to A2), A14 second conditional (B2 to B1), A15 simple passive (B2 to B1), A29 it's/its (B2 to B1), A12 gist listening (B1 to A2), A32 politeness (B2 to B1), A71 (effectively B1), A78 (B2).
   - Pitched too low: A40 (really C2).
   - Because difficulty numbers drive adaptive routing and scoring, these errors will misplace students.
3. **Too much C1 and too many cheap C1 items.** Eight items are tagged C1, but five of them are solvable by elimination (A19, A26) or are exposure items (A21), or are far beyond A1-B2 (A40 at C2, A35 dense read-aloud). They add noise and discourage teens. A1-B2 placement needs more B1/B2 range, not C1.
4. **Skill labels do not match the tasks.** A27-A32 ("writing" and "speaking") are grammar or pragmatic recognition items. A13, A19, A21, A65 are labelled grammar or vocabulary incorrectly. The scores for "speaking" and "writing" are therefore not valid evidence of those skills. Say so in the report, or use real tasks.
5. **Three-way redundancy.**
   - be-verb: A01, A04, A41 and A45.
   - Past simple with a time marker: A05, A27, A49, A53.
   - Inversion: A20, A77, A79.
   - Phone/school topic: A38, A54, A75.
   - Rain/weather: A06, A34, A51, A59, A67.
   - Movie: 7 items.
   - Synonym-matching vocabulary: A13, A22, A23, A24, A25.
   - Opinion-contrast listening: A17 and A76.
   - Family self-introduction reading: A36 and A47.
6. **Distractor quality is uneven.**
   - Many items rely on silly options (A07 "between", A31, A32 "soonly", A19, A26, A38).
   - Several keys are cued by the key being the only positive or advanced word (A19, A22, A25, A26, A78).
   - The key is the longest option in 31 of 79 items (39%).
7. **Cognate fairness.** Romance speakers are advantaged on enormous (A24), meticulous (A25), skepticism (A78), maintain/contain/obtain/retain (A74) and postpone (A73). Arabic and Turkish speakers are not.
8. **Second defensible answers.** A02 (ate), A07 (in/at), A59 (is raining), A39 (writer vs critics). Marginal cases are A08 (I'm liking), A16 (look for), A57 (takes) and A72 (already started).

## What is missing for A1-B2 teens

- **Grammar:** articles a/an/the (critical for Arabic/Turkish), can/can't, possessive adjectives and 's, imperatives, prepositions of time (in/on/at), countable/uncountable beyond "any", past continuous, comparatives of long adjectives and superlatives, will vs going to, reported statements, passive modals, wish, a real B2 linker (despite, however), quantifiers (few/little).
- **Functional language:** greetings, thanks, requests, invitations, apologies, asking the way, shopping. Only one such item exists (A31).
- **Vocabulary range:** school, daily routine, food, free time, travel, health and technology at A2-B1. Current vocabulary is mostly synonym or antonym matching and few collocations or word-building items.
- **Reading:** real-world texts (signs, notices, messages, timetables); a B1 text with a detail trap; a B2 text with inference.
- **Listening:** dialogues, numbers and prices, a B1 gist dialogue.

## The 10 most urgent fixes

1. **Randomize option order** at render time (key in B 58%, never D). Keep sorted options (A55, A46) fixed.
2. **Re-tag mis-levelled items** (A13, A14, A15, A16, A18, A12, A29, A32, A71, A78, A28, A40) so adaptive routing does not mislead.
3. **Fix items with a second correct answer:** A02 (remove "ate"), A07 (in/at), A59 ("is raining"), A39 (writer vs critics).
4. **Replace the redundant or low-value items** (A01, A30, A47, A49, A77, A79, A17) with articles, possessives, functional language, a real-world sign, a B2 wish and a decision listening item.
5. **Remove or replace the C1 elimination items** (A19, A26, A21, A25, A24, A40) with context-based items with same-category distractors.
6. **Fix distractors that are silly or cue the key** (A12, A34, A31, A32, A38, A66, A75). Equalise lengths: the key is the longest in 31 items.
7. **Correct skill labels:** A27-A32 are grammar/pragmatics, not writing/speaking; or replace with real tasks (a short typed sentence or a recorded read-aloud).
8. **American English throughout:** film to movie (A12, A27, A55), cinema to movie theater, Grey to Gray (A33). Avoid dog pets (A33) and military idiom (A21).
9. **Reduce topic repetition:** weather, movies and phones account for 15+ items. Spread over school, food, travel, hobbies, health.
10. **Improve the listening scripts:** A35 should be paraphrased and spoken-style; A34's trap should use the real "light rain after four" detail; A12 should be re-levelled; keep A55's time trap (legitimate), and check the recorded numbers and times by ear.

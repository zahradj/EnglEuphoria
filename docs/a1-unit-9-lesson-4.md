## The two new games

**★ Design My Room (`design-room`)** — the signature, used twice (PPP inside one game):
- *Listen mode (controlled):* Wim gives 5 instructions with between / in front of / next to; the child drags (or taps then taps)
  the sticker to the spot; a wrong spot shakes back and Wim says it again. Things stay where they were put.
- *Free mode (produce):* the child picks 5 stickers and places them anywhere; then a sentence bar ("There is a … / There are two …
  / The … is between the … and the …") helps them describe the room aloud, and Wim says the matching sentence back for each thing.
- **Research (mechanics only):** Teach-This "Inside my bedroom" and "Is there a…?" guessing game; BrainPOP "describe and draw"
  partner task; Altopedia floor-plan drawing; Toca Boca Home Designer (free decorating).
- **Better than them:** the room is the child's own design, so the sentences are true sentences about something they made; the
  listen round checks the PLACE word (two plants, two spots), not just the noun; the teacher can drive it live.

**Lost Property Box (`lost-property`)** — whose / 's: one thing at a time pops out (crown, ball, book, hat), the owner is heard
("Whose ball is it?"); the child drags it to Wim, Cat-cat, Pip or Mia and says "It's Cat-cat's ball." Mechanic from coursebook
"whose…? / possessive 's" practice (Cambridge *Power Up 1*); better: each owner reacts, and things the child gave back stay
with their owner.

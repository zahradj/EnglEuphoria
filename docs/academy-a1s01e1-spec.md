# A1-S01-E1 "Who Are You?" — lesson spec v3 (owner redo, 2026-10-09)

Built with `.claude/skills/academy-lesson-craft`. Research: `docs/research/academy-lesson-craft-research.md`, `docs/research/academy-a1s01e1-research.md`.

**Why v3:** the owner liked the player but rejected the content ("it doesn't serve any purpose or any objective"; some words not understandable; the student must read and repeat the conversation before they own the pattern). v3 starts from one performance the student can really do at the end.

## 1. Objective (owner decision 2026-10-09; overrides the generic Cold-Open objective of the blueprint template)

**By the end I can introduce myself to a new person: say my name, my age, where I am from and one thing I like, and ask "How old are you?" and "Where are you from?" back.** Stretch: say who is in my family.

CEFR A1 (spoken interaction/production): "can introduce him/herself and ask and answer questions about personal details", as a short rehearsed statement with learned frames. The Season can-do already says "I can say hello, say my name, age and country, and ask someone the same."

Blueprint facts kept: 60 minutes, run of show Check-in 5 · Remember? 7 · The Drop 10 · Notice & Build 10 · Energiser 4 · Mission 12 · Release 7 · Wrap 5; cast Vee, Ava, Theo (no Mia); A1 supports (full frames, L1 hint, 0.8x); Mission with Take 1 → feedback → Take 2 and Chill/Normal/Push; Release private first; Clue 1 of 8. **Deviation (owner):** the objective, and the part names on screen follow the owner's stages; the item list is the owner's introduction vocabulary (12 words; the blueprint cap is 14).

## 2. Success criteria (the Wrap tick-list and the teacher rubric)

1. I said my name: "My name is ___."
2. I said my age: "I am ___." (a number)
3. I said where I am from: "I am from ___."
4. I said one thing I like: "I like ___."
5. I asked back: "How old are you?" and "Where are you from?"
6. (stretch) I said who is in my family: "I have a ___ and a ___."

## 3. Exit task (Release, private first)

Fill my introduction card (age, country, hobby, family), then say or type my introduction **with the model hidden** (the model line comes back only on "Show me"): "Hi! My name is ___. I am ___. I am from ___. I like ___. How old are you?" Evidence: card complete, criteria ticked by the student, hints used, "I said it" count, "Show me" used or not.

## 4. Language inventory (exactly what the exit task needs)

- Words with picture + meaning (12): name, age, country, hobby, family, mother, father, brother, sister, football, music, games.
- Frames: Hi! My name is ___. · Nice to meet you. · How old are you? · I am ___. · Where are you from? · I am from ___. · I like ___. · I have a ___ and a ___.
- Student's own: a name or nickname (any), a number 11–18, a country (typed), a hobby (tap, 3 taught + 3 glossed with pictures).

## 5. Route (owner's order) and alignment

| Part (min) | Owner stage | Screens | Trains |
|---|---|---|---|
| Check-in (5) | Say hello | Club-lobby scene, Vee waves; today's goal card (the empty introduction card + criteria); student types name and picks a mood; Ava and Theo greet them by name | 1 |
| Remember? (7) | **1. Learn the words** | 3 sets x 4 pictured cards (picture, word, meaning, example), each set checked by tapping the picture for the word | 1-6 |
| The Drop (10) | **2. Read the story** | Full-page scene, Ava + Theo (Vee comments) act a 12-line meeting; every line is read, then said ("I said it" or type it); 2 understanding checks | 1-6 |
| Notice & Build (10) | **3. Practise the words, 4. Complete the conversation** | Match word↔picture (6) · match word↔meaning (6) · complete the conversation x2 (word bank, 3-4 gaps each) · build "I am from Brazil." · build "How old are you?" | 1-6 |
| Energiser (4) | quick game | Odd one out x2 (family / hobbies / countries) · hobby would-you-rather | 4, 6 |
| Mission (12) | **5. Say it by heart** | Read-and-repeat 4 key lines with the key words hidden, then only the first word shown · pick Chill/Normal/Push · Take 1 role-play with Theo (answer with your own facts) · feedback · Take 2 (add the questions back) | 1-5 |
| Release (7) | **6. Introduce yourself** | Exit task (model hidden) · Ava reacts | 1-6 |
| Wrap (5) | check | 5-6 can-do ticks (Yes / Almost / Not yet), homework "introduce yourself to one friend", clue 1 of 8 tease | 1-6 |

Alignment rule (test): every interactive screen carries a `trains` tag; every criterion 1-5 has >= 3 screens and its last screen is production (Mission or Release); the exit task uses only inventory items that were practised >= 3 times.

## 6. Research (>= 3 benchmarks, mechanic taken, never content)

1. **Cambridge/Oxford A1 Unit 1** (Touchstone 1, English Unlimited Starter): match greetings to pictures → fill the gaps in a dialogue → order introduction sentences → pair role-play → write about yourself. Taken: the whole practice ladder. Better: every step is tagged to a criterion and the exit is private and model-hidden.
2. **ABA English**: film first, then imitate the characters, dub, then write. Taken: story as a scene, imitation of the characters' lines. Better: line-by-line read-and-repeat with fading support, and we run it live with a teacher.
3. **Busuu / Babbel / Duolingo**: shadowing-style listen-and-repeat with record, turn-taking dialogue practice, picture matching and tap-to-build from lesson one. Taken: say-it step on every line (type-it as the comfort option), picture match, tile builder. Better: the dialogue is the single pool every exercise is built from.
Evidence and limits: `docs/research/academy-lesson-craft-research.md` (recitation helps beginners' formulaic expressions in the short term; PPP-style scaffolding with a task at the end for A1; no controlled study found on fading cloze).

# Pre-A1 reading-first design note (owner, 2026-10-15)

Rule: `CLAUDE.md` "Pre-A1 is reading-first". Research: `docs/research/academy-pre-a1-reading-research.md`.

## The four steps in every Pre-A1 lesson

| Step | Where (the 8-part run of show) | What the student does | Slide kinds |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Activate | Check-in | taps "I know it" / "New to me" for the words of the day; the teacher sees what is already known | `sort` (no wrong answers) |
| 2 Sounds and blending | Learn, Remember? | hears the sound, blends letters into words, spells them | `teach`, `rhythm`/`hearpick`, `blend`, `spell` |
| 3 Read | Read (11 min) | decodable lines, then a short message from a character; read twice (listen, then read); tap any word to hear it | `reader`, `passage` |
| 4 Word bank | Read, Wrap, Daily 10 | keeps the words they want from the message; plays listen-and-match with the word tiles; the bank comes back on the spaced schedule | `passage` (keep), `match` (word tiles), recall plan |

## Text rules

- A text uses only the sounds taught so far plus the lesson's sight words (`taughtSounds`, `sightWords`, checked by `decodable.ts`); a few lines at first, growing each lesson.
- Teen content: a lock-screen message, a chat, a caption, a label. Never a child's story.
- Decodable lines are the bridge, not the whole diet: the message page is real-looking text with taught words.
- The words a student keeps become **practice items** (Remember? and the Daily 10 return them after 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 lessons).

## Pre-A1 S2 L1 example

Activate: phone, map, pin, tin, in, it. Sounds: i, n. Blend: in, it, pin, tin. Read: "It is a pin. It is a tin." then two short messages from Theo and Ava. Word bank: pin, tin, map, phone, in, it, is.

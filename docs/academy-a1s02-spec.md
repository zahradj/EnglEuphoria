# A1 Season 2 "My Room, My Stuff" — Season plan (2026-10-13)

Owner: "continue with A1", then "yes" to writing the Season 2 spec and building its first lesson. Follows `academy-roadmap-architect`. The Season spec as data lives in `src/curriculum/academy/a1s02.ts` (tested against the lesson blueprints in `__tests__/a1s02.test.ts`); this page is the readable version.
Audience: Academy ages 13-18, A1 (after Season 1 "Who Are You?", so hello, name, age, country, numbers 1-10 are known and revised fast).

## 1. At a glance
| | |
|---|---|
| Theme | **The Lost Charger**: Theo's phone is at 3 % and his charger has vanished from his room. Follow the clues. |
| Cast | Vee (mentor), Theo (lead, 15, Italian), Ava (13, Spanish; she borrowed the charger for the club night) |
| Release | A voice-note tour of my room (30-45 s) |
| Structures | there is / there are (+ a / an / any, plural -s) in E3; in / on / under in E4; next to / behind / in front of / between in E6 |
| Pronunciation | plural endings /s/ /z/ /iz/; /i/ vs /i:/ (it / eat) |
| Can-dos | say what there is in a room and where things are; name objects and gadgets and their colours; ask where something is |

## 2. Eight episodes
| Ep | Name | Items | New language | Mission | Release | Clue |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | Cold Open | 14 | charger, phone, bag, cable, plug, room, mess, lost, find, look, where, here, there, Oh no! | predict where the charger is and say why (1-2 sentences) | one-sentence reaction: My charger is… | A cable under the bed: not Theo's. Whose is it? |

## 3. Sequencing and load
- 80 items over six teaching episodes (at most 14 each); E7 and E8 teach nothing new. Furniture and gadgets (E2) come before the sentences that use them (E3); places (E4) before colours and gadget reading (E5); finer places (E6) last.
- Every word set is followed by two different memorising games (vocabulary rule); picks per episode are in the spec data.
- Recycling: numbers 1-10, hello and name, and countries from Season 1 return in Remember? and in the Mission (There are two chairs; Theo is Italian).

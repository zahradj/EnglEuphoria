# Academy curriculum — Pre-A1

> Generated. 3 Seasons × 8 = **24 live lessons** of 60 minutes (cumulative live hours from Pre-A1: **24**). Plan rule: every lesson has an approved one-to-one plan first (`docs/academy-lesson-plan-template.md`) with research logged in `docs/research/`.

## 1. Level at a glance
- **CEFR:** Pre-A1 is a level of the CEFR Companion Volume (2020) below A1 (confirmed by search; descriptor wording not opened).
- **The student:** A 13-18 student with no or almost no English; may read Arabic script or a Latin script with different sound rules. Needs letters, sounds and first words before A1 lesson 1 ("Sign Me Up").
- **Method:** Blending is the spine: every lesson has a 3-5 minute blend routine and a decodable reading text. Chunks first ("My name is ___"), analysed later. 8 new items per session at most. Teen scenes (phone, lockers, bus stop), never childish.
- **Supports:** Home-language hint on, audio 0.8x, pictures on every word, no timers, "Not yet" wording, private rehearsal, type-instead-of-speak.
- **Input texts:** Decodable lines of 3-6 words, texts of 5 lines.
- **Daily 10:** 10 minutes: hear-and-pick, blend slider, one decodable text with tap-to-hear, one voice note; spaced at 1, 3, 7 days.

## 4. Every Season has the same eight lessons
Cold Open (hook, gist) · Word Lab (vocabulary and pronunciation) · Pattern Lab (grammar noticing) · On Air (real exchange) · Deep Dive (longer text and short writing) · Side Quest (student's pick, light new language) · Remix (zero new language) · Finale (peer task, Release, checkpoint, zero new language). Run of show: standard 60 minutes (Pre-A1 uses its own slower one).

## 5. Pronunciation and L1 priorities
- Short /i/ vs /iː/ (Arabic, Spanish, Italian)
- Final /n/ (French nasalisation)
- Left-to-right letter-by-letter reading (Arabic readers)
- Aspirated /p/ /t/

## 6. Assessment
- Remember? at the start of every lesson
- Season checkpoint in E8 (hear-and-pick letters, sound-to-letter, read-and-match, build own name, say a chunk)
- Pre-A1 exit task: read a 5-sentence profile and answer 5 questions; say 5 sentences about yourself

## 7. To move up to the next level
- Reads 5-sentence decodable profile with at most 2 hints
- Says and writes own name, age and 5 chunks intelligibly
- Knows all 26 letter names and the sounds taught so far

## 8. Lanes (school and exam practice, inside the Season; see `docs/academy-curriculum-gaps-plan.md`)
School lane only (classroom words and instructions); no exam lane.

## 9. Games and video-game mechanics
- **Game mix:** Sound and picture games first: Memory Pairs, Whack-a-Word, Sound Bingo, Simon Says Sounds, Spot the Sound, Tile Stacker (slow), listen-and-match, read-and-match (catalogue 149-168).
- **Video-game mechanics for this level:**
- Loot and crafting (sound tiles to words)
- Rhythm lane
- Escape-room locker code
- Speedrun against your own best (no leaderboards)
- Lists: `docs/academy-games-catalog.md` (168), `docs/academy-video-game-mechanics.md`.

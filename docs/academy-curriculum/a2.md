# Academy curriculum — A2

> Generated. 14 Seasons × 8 = **112 live lessons** of 60 minutes (cumulative live hours from Pre-A1: **248**). Plan rule: every lesson has an approved one-to-one plan first (`docs/academy-lesson-plan-template.md`) with research logged in `docs/research/`.

## 1. Level at a glance
- **CEFR:** A2 (paraphrase): understands frequently used expressions on immediate topics; handles simple routine exchanges; describes in simple terms background, immediate environment and past events.
- **The student:** A student who can manage the basics and now needs past, future, comparison and obligation to talk about real life.
- **Method:** Still model-and-rule, with half frames; one structure per Season (two at most); present perfect held to B1; going to and will in separate Seasons.
- **Supports:** Half frames, tiered hints, audio 0.9x on request.
- **Input texts:** ~80-150 words [target].
- **Daily 10:** 10 minutes of retrieval plus a short text; one voice note per week.

## 4. Every Season has the same eight lessons
Cold Open (hook, gist) · Word Lab (vocabulary and pronunciation) · Pattern Lab (grammar noticing) · On Air (real exchange) · Deep Dive (longer text and short writing) · Side Quest (student's pick, light new language) · Remix (zero new language) · Finale (peer task, Release, checkpoint, zero new language). Run of show: standard 60 minutes (Pre-A1 uses its own slower one).

## 5. Pronunciation and L1 priorities
- Past -ed endings /t/ /d/ /id/
- Irregular verb forms
- Article use
- Question word order (Turkish, Arabic)
- Stress in compound nouns

Season pronunciation focus:
- A2-S01: -ed endings /t/ /d/ /ɪd/; was / were in fast speech (weak forms)
- A2-S02: vowel changes in irregular verbs (go / went, buy / bought); did you ... linking /dɪdʒu/ (listening focus)
- A2-S03: weak "than" /ðən/; word stress in adjectives (expensive, comfortable)
- A2-S04: "going to" reduced to /ˈɡəʊɪŋ tə/ in connected speech (listening); sentence stress in plans
- A2-S05: stress in body-part and symptom words (stomach, headache); should / shouldn't: the weak vowel
- A2-S06: "mustn't" - silent t and final cluster; sentence stress in rules
- A2-S07: stress in food words (chocolate, vegetable); linking in "a lot of"
- A2-S08: weak "to" /tə/ in "I go there to study"; number and time stress in announcements
- A2-S09: contracted 'll /l/ ('I'll, she'll); won't vs want (vowel length)
- A2-S10: was / were /wəz/ /wə/ in connected speech; stress in two-syllable weather words
- A2-S11: -ly adverb stress (carefully, quickly); rising tone for yes/no questions
- A2-S12: intonation in polite suggestions (Let's go! / Shall we ...?); dates: ordinal numbers stress
- A2-S13: polite rising intonation on Could you ...? / Can I ...?; weak "as ... as" /əz/ in connected speech
- A2-S14: stress on the first syllable of SOMEbody / ANYthing / NOthing; present simple -s vs present continuous -ing endings

## 11. Season details
### A2-S01 Last Weekend
*Ava posted photos from a weekend nobody remembers her having. Piece together what really happened.*
- I can say what I did and where I was at a time in the past.
- I can understand a short story about past events.
- Lexical fields: weekend activities; time expressions for the past; places and events; feelings
- Skins: social, football, music, travel

### A2-S02 Travel Trouble
*Theo's school trip had a lost passport, a missed train and one very strange taxi. Find out what he did next.*
- I can tell a simple story about a trip, using common past verbs.
- I can ask and answer questions about what happened.
- Lexical fields: travel and transport; holiday problems; common irregular verbs; places abroad
- Skins: travel, gaming, film, food

### A2-S03 Gadget Wars
*Two rival phone brands are fighting over Mia's choice for her birthday. Compare them and settle the argument.*
- I can compare two or more things and say which is best.
- I can give a short reason for my choice.
- Lexical fields: technology and features; adjectives for quality and size; prices and money
- Skins: tech, gaming, fashion, music

### A2-S04 Plans and Dreams
*Vee asks everyone to hand in a plan for the summer. Ava's plan is very bold. Can you make a better one?*
- I can say what I am going to do and when.
- I can ask others about their plans and understand the answers.
- Lexical fields: holiday and summer activities; future time expressions; places to visit
- Skins: travel, music, football, art

### A2-S05 Body and Health
*Theo has been playing games all night and feels awful. Use your best advice to get him back on his feet.*
- I can say what is wrong with me and understand simple health advice.
- I can give advice to a friend.
- Lexical fields: parts of the body; common illnesses and symptoms; sleep, food and exercise
- Skins: fitness, gaming, food, football

### A2-S06 House Rules
*Mia has to write the rules for the school gaming club. Can you help her make rules everyone will follow?*
- I can say what you must and must not do in a place or game.
- I can say what I have to do at home or at school.
- Lexical fields: school and club rules; safety signs; household duties; games and sports rules
- Skins: gaming, football, tech, fitness

### A2-S07 Food Cultures
*Vee's friend is hosting an international dinner and needs a menu. How much of everything do you need for ten people?*
- I can talk about how much or how many of something there is.
- I can follow and give a simple recipe.
- Lexical fields: ingredients and quantities; cooking verbs; meals and customs around the world
- Skins: food, travel, fitness, film

### A2-S08 Getting Around
*Ava is new in the city and has two hours to get across it. Plan her route before she misses her interview.*
- I can explain how to get from one place to another and why I go there.
- I can understand simple travel announcements and instructions.
- Lexical fields: transport and tickets; city places and buildings; travel announcements
- Skins: travel, tech, music, nature

### A2-S09 Tomorrow's World
*Vee opens a time capsule from 2010 that predicted what 2030 would be like. Check its predictions with Mia and write your own for the next capsule.*
- I can make simple predictions and say what I think will happen.
- I can make quick decisions and offer to help.
- Lexical fields: technology and the future; jobs of the future; weather and environment
- Skins: science, tech, gaming, nature

### A2-S10 Storm Warning
*Ava, Theo, Mia and Vee were camping when the storm hit. Each of them remembers a different version of that night.*
- I can say what was happening at a moment in the past.
- I can link two short sentences with so and because.
- Lexical fields: weather and nature; camping and outdoor equipment; emergencies and feelings
- Skins: nature, film, gaming, travel

### A2-S11 Dream Jobs
*Career Day is coming and Theo has no idea what he wants to be. Interview three people and help him decide.*
- I can describe a job and the skills it needs.
- I can say how well someone does something.
- Lexical fields: jobs and workplaces; personal qualities; skills and abilities
- Skins: business, tech, fitness, art, animals

### A2-S12 Event Planners
*The school festival is in two weeks and Vee's committee has no ideas. Help Ava and Theo make suggestions, take decisions and save the day.*
- I can make, accept and refuse suggestions politely.
- I can write a short invitation with the key information.
- Lexical fields: festivals and events; dates and times; food, music and decorations
- Skins: music, food, art, fashion, football

### A2-S13 Money Talks
*Mia is saving for a pair of headphones, but her bank app shows less money than she expected. Ask polite questions, compare prices and find out where the missing cash went.*
- I can ask for things, for permission and for help politely (Can I ...? Could you ...? May I ...?).
- I can compare two prices or products (as cheap as, not as good as).
- I can understand a short text about pocket money and part-time jobs.
- Lexical fields: money, saving and spending; pocket money and part-time jobs; online shopping and prices
- Skins: business, tech, fashion, gaming

### A2-S14 Stay Safe Online
*Theo's account sends strange messages that he never wrote. Vee and Ava follow the clues, one rule at a time, to find out who knows his password and how to lock the door again.*
- I can say that nobody, somebody or nothing is true or not (There is nothing wrong. Nobody knows my password.).
- I can say what I usually do online and what I am doing right now.
- I can give simple rules and advice about passwords and privacy.
- Lexical fields: accounts, passwords and privacy; sharing photos and messages; online rules and problems
- Skins: social, tech, gaming, film

# Academy curriculum — A1

> Generated. 12 Seasons × 8 = **96 live lessons** of 60 minutes (cumulative live hours from Pre-A1: **136**). Plan rule: every lesson has an approved one-to-one plan first (`docs/academy-lesson-plan-template.md`) with research logged in `docs/research/`.

## 1. Level at a glance
- **CEFR:** A1 (paraphrase): understands and uses familiar everyday expressions and very basic phrases; introduces self and others; asks and answers simple questions about personal details.
- **The student:** A teen who has met hello, numbers and countries at school: A1 revises these fast and spends the time on what school did not teach. Never childish.
- **Method:** Model first (I do / we do / you do), rule stated plainly before practice, student teach-back. Mission with Take 1 / Take 2. Eight-episode Season: Cold Open, Word Lab, Pattern Lab, On Air, Deep Dive, Side Quest, Remix, Finale.
- **Supports:** Maximum: full sentence frames, home-language hint, audio 0.8x.
- **Input texts:** ~30-60 words [target].
- **Daily 10:** 10 minutes of spaced retrieval and one speaking task (voice note).

## 4. Every Season has the same eight lessons
Cold Open (hook, gist) · Word Lab (vocabulary and pronunciation) · Pattern Lab (grammar noticing) · On Air (real exchange) · Deep Dive (longer text and short writing) · Side Quest (student's pick, light new language) · Remix (zero new language) · Finale (peer task, Release, checkpoint, zero new language). Run of show: standard 60 minutes (Pre-A1 uses its own slower one).

## 5. Pronunciation and L1 priorities
- Plural endings /s/ /z/ /iz/
- /θ/ vs /s/ (listen first)
- be-verb omission (Arabic, Turkish)
- Subject pronoun dropping (Spanish, Italian, Turkish)
- False friends

Season pronunciation focus:
- A1-S01: the alphabet (letter names that sound alike: e/i, b/p); word stress in country names
- A1-S02: plural endings /s/ /z/ /iz/; the /ɪ/ vs /iː/ contrast (it / eat)
- A1-S03: -ing endings; sentence stress on key words
- A1-S04: the /θ/ and /s/ in "thanks / sandwich" (listen first); rising tone in polite questions
- A1-S05: third-person -s endings /s/ /z/ /iz/; the /h/ sound in "he / has / hour"
- A1-S06: the /ð/ sound in "mother, father, brother" (aim for intelligible); stress in adjective + noun phrases
- A1-S07: stress in compound nouns (bus stop, car park); clear final consonants (turn, left)
- A1-S08: can /kən/ vs can't /kɑːnt/ (the vowel carries the meaning); the /ŋ/ in "singing, dancing"
- A1-S09: teens vs tens (13/30, 14/40) - stress on the right syllable; the /ɪ/ vs /iː/ contrast (this / these)
- A1-S10: -ing and weak "are" in "What are you doing?"; falling intonation in wh-questions
- A1-S11: Wednesday /ˈwenzdeɪ/ and Thursday /θ/ (silent d; th awareness for fr, tr, it); weak "to" /tə/ in "want to / need to"
- A1-S12: a vs an linked to the next word ("an_old_watch"); the /h/ in him / her (fr speakers drop it) and the vowel in them / us

## 11. Season details
### A1-S01 Who Are You?
*A fake profile has appeared in Ava's group chat. Who made it - and can you spot what is wrong with it?*
- I can say hello, say my name, age and country, and ask someone the same.
- I can spell my name and read a short online profile.
- Lexical fields: personal information; countries and nationalities; numbers 0-20; the alphabet; classroom language
- Skins: gaming, music, football, social

### A1-S02 My Room, My Stuff
*Theo's charger keeps vanishing. Follow the clues around his room to find out where it went.*
- I can say what there is in a room and where things are.
- I can name common objects and gadgets and say what colour they are.
- Lexical fields: furniture and rooms; gadgets and everyday objects; colours; plural nouns
- Skins: tech, gaming, art, animals

### A1-S03 Free Time Club
*At the Club Fair, Ava needs three members before Friday or her club closes. Can you help her find them?*
- I can say what I like and do in my free time.
- I can ask a classmate what they like doing and understand the answer.
- Lexical fields: hobbies and sports; music and media; places for free time
- Skins: football, music, gaming, anime, art

### A1-S04 Café Night
*Mia is running a pop-up café for one night with a tiny budget. Help her take orders and keep the customers happy.*
- I can ask for food and drink politely and understand the price.
- I can say what I want and what I have not got.
- Lexical fields: food and drink; menu items and prices; quantities and containers
- Skins: food, fitness, travel, film

### A1-S05 A Day in My Life
*Mia is filming a 'day in my life' vlog challenge. Can you describe your day better than hers?*
- I can say what I do every day and at what time.
- I can tell the time and talk about my school day.
- Lexical fields: daily routine verbs; school subjects and timetable; times of day and days of the week
- Skins: social, football, music, fitness

### A1-S06 Family and Friends
*A family photo from Theo's grandparents has no names on the back. Work out who is who from the clues.*
- I can say who the people in my life are and describe what they look like.
- I can say what people have and whose things they are.
- Lexical fields: family members; appearance and personality adjectives; ages and birthdays
- Skins: animals, film, anime, fashion

### A1-S07 Treasure Map Town
*Vee has hidden a treasure somewhere in town and left a map with missing words. Fill in the directions to reach it.*
- I can name places in a town and say where they are.
- I can ask for and give simple directions.
- Lexical fields: places in town; directions and landmarks; transport
- Skins: travel, gaming, nature, football

### A1-S08 Can Do!
*Theo secretly entered the school talent show. Find out what each friend can do before the curtain goes up.*
- I can say what I can and cannot do.
- I can ask others what they can do and join two ideas with and or but.
- Lexical fields: abilities and skills; sports and performing arts; common verbs of action
- Skins: music, football, gaming, art, fitness

### A1-S09 Market Day
*Ava has a stall at the vintage market and ten pounds to spend. Can you bargain better than she can?*
- I can ask how much something is and say what I want to buy.
- I can point to things and say which one I mean.
- Lexical fields: clothes and accessories; colours and sizes; numbers to 100 and money
- Skins: fashion, tech, music, gaming

### A1-S10 Right Now
*Vee is going live from the town square and needs a co-host who can describe what is happening, right now.*
- I can say what is happening at this moment.
- I can ask and answer simple questions with who, what, where, when and why.
- Lexical fields: actions and activities; weather and seasons; places and events
- Skins: social, football, travel, film, nature

### A1-S11 Plan the Weekend
*Mia has a spare ticket for Saturday night but the message she sent is full of gaps: no day, no time, no place. Fill in the plan before Theo books something else.*
- I can say what I want and need to do on a day or at a time.
- I can invite someone, and accept or politely say no, giving a day and a time.
- Lexical fields: days, months and dates; weekend activities; invitations and replies
- Skins: social, film, music, football, food

### A1-S12 Lost and Found
*Ava runs the school lost-and-found box and nothing has a label. Theo swears the black bag is his. Describe each object and work out whose it is.*
- I can name and describe my things using a, an, the and plural nouns.
- I can ask whose something is, and use me, him, her, us and them correctly.
- Lexical fields: personal belongings; materials, shapes and sizes; giving and returning things
- Skins: tech, fashion, gaming, travel

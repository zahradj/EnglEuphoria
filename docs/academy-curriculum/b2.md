# Academy curriculum — B2

> Generated. 18 Seasons × 8 = **144 live lessons** of 60 minutes (cumulative live hours from Pre-A1: **536**). Plan rule: every lesson has an approved one-to-one plan first (`docs/academy-lesson-plan-template.md`) with research logged in `docs/research/`.

## 1. Level at a glance
- **CEFR:** B2 (paraphrase): understands the main ideas of complex text; interacts with fluency and spontaneity; produces clear, detailed text and explains a viewpoint with advantages and disadvantages.
- **The student:** An upper-intermediate student who defends opinions, summarises and mediates, and prepares for study in English.
- **Method:** Student-led tasks, opinion defence, summarising and mediation every Season; supports off by default (optional).
- **Supports:** None by default; optional on request.
- **Input texts:** ~300-500 words [target].
- **Daily 10:** 10 minutes plus an extensive-reading and listening target.

## 4. Every Season has the same eight lessons
Cold Open (hook, gist) · Word Lab (vocabulary and pronunciation) · Pattern Lab (grammar noticing) · On Air (real exchange) · Deep Dive (longer text and short writing) · Side Quest (student's pick, light new language) · Remix (zero new language) · Finale (peer task, Release, checkpoint, zero new language). Run of show: standard 60 minutes (Pre-A1 uses its own slower one).

### B2-S01 Been There, Doing That
*Everyone in Vee's class has been working on a personal project all term. Whose has actually moved forward?*
**Big question:** How can I report what I have been doing on my project and what it has achieved?
- I can describe an activity that has been going on and its present results.
- I can give a clear, detailed account of progress to an audience.

| Lesson | Chapter | Sub-question | One difficulty that rises |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | **Whose project moved?** | Whose project has really moved forward this term? | Recognise: follow who has been doing what in a project update (gist) |
| E2 | **Goals, effort, progress** | Which words describe project goals, effort and progress? | Controlled: say and use words that belong together in a project |
| E3 | **Been doing, for how long?** | How do I say what has been going on, and for how long, in the present perfect continuous? | Controlled: build accurate sentences about ongoing effort |
| E4 | **The progress check-in** | How do I ask a teammate about their progress and explain the results of mine? | Guided: a short check-in with a partner, causes and results included |
| E5 | **The long team message** | What does a long message from a team member say about effort and results? | More input and reasoning: a longer text, then summarise the progress for the group |
| E6 | **My own project** | What have I been working on in the world I chose, and what has it led to? | Free choice: my own project, my own words, little planning |
| E7 | **Remix: who has progressed?** | Can I describe ongoing effort and its results without help? | Fluency on known language only: no new items, no prompts |
| E8 | **My progress report** | How can I report what I have been doing on my project and what it has achieved? | Release: the hardest task, a clear detailed progress report, spoken and written |

- Lexical fields: projects and goals; effort and progress; study and sport
- Mediation: Summarise for the group the progress described in a long message from a team member.
- Skins: fitness, tech, music, art, gaming

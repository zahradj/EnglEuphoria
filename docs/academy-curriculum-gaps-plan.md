# Academy curriculum gaps — plan (2026-10-14)

From `docs/research/academy-curriculum-benchmarks.md` (search summaries only; primary pages were not all reachable) and `academy-curriculum-primary-source-check.md`. The 70-Season / 560-session sizing stays; these gaps are delivered **inside** it, mostly through E6 Side Quest (the student's pick, light new language) and the Season skins. Status: plan, owner may change any row.

| # | Gap | How we close it (no new Seasons) | Where | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | No exam-prep or school-English bridge | E6 "lanes": **School lane** (A1–B1: language for class tasks, instructions, tests) and **Exam lane** (A2–C1: Cambridge-style task types for Reading, Writing, Listening, Speaking as practice, not coaching to a named exam) | E6 of every Season; lane choice is the student's | planned |
| 2 | Hours below Cambridge guided learning hours at B2/C1 (live 432 / 560 vs 500–600 / 700–800) | Do not claim equivalence. Count Daily 10 (about 10 min × days) as guided practice and state the total honestly in the parent report; revisit if the owner adds sessions | parent report, marketing copy | planned; claim limited |
| 3 | Teen topics no Season names: money and first jobs, online safety, mental health, climate, friendship and peer pressure, study plans, volunteering | Use them as **My World skins** and E5 Deep Dive texts in B1–C1 Seasons; add a topic to each Season's `lexicalFields`/`skins` when the Season is built | Season specs when built | planned |
| 4 | Mediation starts only at B2 Season 15 | Add one A2 and one B1 mediation task (relay a message, summarise a short text) as E6 options; A2 optional | E6 | planned |
| 5 | No dedicated sound lesson / recorded pronunciation check | Pronunciation focus per Season already exists; add a 3-minute recorded check in E4 and a comprehensibility (not accent) rubric, per `academy-curriculum-teen-sla-and-l1-gaps.md` | E4, rubric | planned |
| 6 | One linear ladder, no per-skill level | Keep one ladder; record per-skill can-do evidence in diagnostics (`academy-learner-diagnostics`) | diagnostics | planned |
| 7 | No mid-level check or parent CEFR report | Big Remix (E7 of even Seasons) already checks; add a one-page parent progress report from can-do evidence | parent page | planned |
| 8 | No culture strand | Fold one cultural comparison into each Season's E5 text (home country vs target-culture practice) | E5 | planned |

Every lane/mechanic added to a Season follows the CLAUDE.md research rule (sources logged) and names its games and a video-game mechanic (`docs/academy-video-game-mechanics.md`).

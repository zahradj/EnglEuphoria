## Decisions taken from the research

- **Ladder:** Pre-A1 (Starters) → A1 (Movers) → A2 (Flyers / Key for Schools) → B1 (Preliminary for Schools) → B2 (First for Schools format, child-safe topics only — the Playground is ages 4-12).
- **No grammar twice as new:** A2 opens with what A1 ended on (routines, past) and moves to past continuous, futures, conditionals, present perfect; B1 adds deduction, passives, relative clauses, present perfect continuous, second conditional, used to / past perfect, reported speech; B2 adds narrative tenses, future perfect/continuous, past modals, all passives + causative, contrast linkers, reporting verbs, wish + third/mixed conditionals, non-defining clauses, verb patterns.
- **Reading strand:** phonics ends at A1/A2 (long vowels, silent letters); B1 and B2 move to word building and word formation, which the B1/B2 exams test.
- **Writing strand:** grows with the exams — A2 30-40 words, B1 80-100, B2 120-150 (kept below the adult First 140-190 for 10-12-year-olds).
- **Limits:** the official Flyers list (handbook p. 77) and the English Grammar / Vocabulary Profile could not be opened from the container; check them before the A2 unit plans are approved.

**Video-game mechanic: Loot and crafting** (*Minecraft*-style crafting grid). Evidence: existing language mods ([MineLingo](https://modrinth.com/mod/minelingo), [QuizCraft Pro](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/quizcraft-pro)) **gate** items behind quizzes and a small Indonesian study reports gains with Minecraft; **no source found for a crafting grid that combines words** [U]. So the "Word Workbench" (combine a feeling + a reason, a verb + a noun into a crafted sentence card) is our own design and must be piloted; no effect is claimed.

## 3. What this changes in the plan
- Whole-chunk-first collocations, no reassembling broken phrases (Boers).
- Every word is heard, said, matched, sorted and used in a sentence about the student (Nation, form-meaning-use).
- The -ed / -ing contrast is a short noticing task (feelings vs causes), not new vocabulary.
- Limits: one-to-one use is undocumented for most of these; pilot with a real teacher and student before relying on them.

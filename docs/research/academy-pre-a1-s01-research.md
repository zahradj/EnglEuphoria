# Research log: Pre-A1 Season P1 "Hello, letters" (2026-10-12)

Benchmarks (mechanics only, never content): **Letters and Sounds (UK)**, **Jolly Phonics**, **Ladybird Readers Starter (Pre-A1)**, **Oxford Phonics World**, **Cambridge Pre A1 Starters** (topics), **Duolingo-style letter games** (not verified here).

| Source | What it gives | Quality / limit |
|---|---|---|
| [Phase Two Phonics, Letters and Sounds](https://chrishall.essex.sch.uk/essex/primary/chrishall/arenas/english/wiki/pages/phonics/phase2phonics), [Letters and Sounds overview](https://www.great-chart.kent.sch.uk/assets/Documents/Attachments/Curriculum/English/Letters-and-Sounds-Phonics-Overview-KS1.pdf) | Set 1 = s a t p, Set 2 = i n m d, Set 3 = g o c k; blend as soon as a set is taught; tricky words (the, to, I, no, go) taught alongside | school pages, mirrors the official programme; children |
| Jolly Phonics order (s a t i p n first) | frequent, easy sounds; many words early (about 40 words after the first group) | aggregator pages, low quality; check the official material |
| [Ladybird Readers Starter](https://www.ladybirdeducation.co.uk/?p=10622) | Pre-A1 readers pair a phonics version with a sight-word version of the same story | ages 4-7; method only |
| [Oxford Phonics World](https://www.oupjapan.co.jp/en/node/63878) | phonics course used before or with a main course; levels of 75-100+ words | app figures, children |
| [Korkowski 2013](https://digitalcommons.hamline.edu/hse_all/529), [Torres 2025](https://stars.library.ucf.edu/jele/vol17/iss1/1) | low-literacy adult ESL learner built decoding while learning spoken English; phonics built into a beginner curriculum linked to gains and completion | one case study / one institution |
| [Colorado decodable text guidance](https://www.cde.state.co.us/coloradoliteracy/decodable_leveled_text), [McQuillan 2022](https://llt.org.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/issue-21-The-Case-Against-Decodable-Texts.pdf) | decodable text supports accurate decoding for beginners / a review says early-reading research does not support it | opposing views, mostly young children |
| [TESOL study guide](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/tesol-certificate/adult-literacy-vocab-pronunciation/adult-print-literacy-decoding) | separate pre-literate and non-literate learners; teach print concepts with adult realia; Language Experience texts from learners' own speech | study guide, not peer-reviewed |

**Taken:** small first sound set, blend at once, tricky/sight words alongside, own name as the first text, systematic order, teen realia (club, phone, names), a paired "decode / sight word" idea for the reader page. **Not taken:** child stories, songs, characters. **Mechanic upgrade vs the benchmarks:** the blend slider with letter tiles that click together, tap-to-hear on every word, and the Name Wall as a visible reward (each learned letter lights a tile).
**Not found:** research on adolescent non-literate ESL learners specifically (SLIFE), Cambridge's own Pre-A1 syllabus numbers, a published teen decodable reader.

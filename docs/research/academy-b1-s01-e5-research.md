| Headline match (match a headline to a paragraph) | exam board (Cambridge) | gist | **CHOSEN as the skim step** (crit 1) |
| Reading race / scavenger hunt | classroom | scan for answers on a timer | REJECTED: timer, comfort rule |

| Spelling-based search (type a word) | video game (Her Story) | typing keywords | ADAPTED: tap chips, no spelling barrier |

Chosen per criterion: 1 skim (headline match, Archive Search); 2 detail (Archive Search); 3 judge (Fact-Check Wall); 4 relay (spoken summary Mission); 5 write and fix (Red Pen).

## Video-game mechanic
**Database keyword search + evidence board** (Her Story + Obra Dinn). Student in English: reads clues, taps a keyword, reads the passages that come back, pins the facts, says what the article says.

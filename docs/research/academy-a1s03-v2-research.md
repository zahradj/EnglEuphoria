# Research: A1 S3 E1 version 2 "The Missing Poster" (be original, 2026-10-14)

Owner (2026-10-14): the lesson repeats lesson one of unit one; be more creative and original, new scenarios, scenes, images and games. Four web searches (standard mode) plus a read-only survey of the Playground hub and player (hub separation: patterns are read and re-implemented in Academy files, never imported). Mechanics only, never content. Not retrieved: British Council, Busuu or Cambridge pages.

Decisions: (1) a new scene (fair-night) and new games (Explore, Sort) instead of the Season 1 opening (badge-convo, brief card, who-said, border booth, padlock, find-the-wrong-line); (2) the lesson keeps the teaching spine (context, model, stated rule, controlled practice, free practice, produce, teach-back) because the owner requires it; (3) pictures are Canva cut-out stickers (35 % purple) and a full-bleed Canva scene.

Next steps from the Playground survey, not built yet: a fog-of-war Season path in the library, a Season checkpoint screen with a per-skill result, a collectible Season album, a skinnable shell (Explorer 13-15 / Studio 16-18), an Academy variety gate (at most 2 identical games in a row, a different setting from the previous lesson), a crash-safe "safe mode".

Design decisions from the research: (1) one story (fact-checking a fake profile) gives a reason for statements, negatives and questions together; (2) wrong forms are the distractors in tile builds and gap-fills; (3) a question is shown as the same words swapped; (4) short answers (Yes, I am. No, he is not.) are taught and practised as their own chunks; (5) the exit is typing five checked sentences, model hidden. Contractions (I'm, isn't) are accepted in writing but the lesson teaches the full forms.
Not found: controlled studies on tile-building or fact-check games for A1 teens; evidence here is design reasoning plus error-type data.

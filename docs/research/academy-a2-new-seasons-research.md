- **E8 six-strand checkpoint idea:** listening (price announcement), reading (short pocket-money text), speaking (shop role-play, Release), writing (a 3-line chat message asking politely), vocabulary (money words), grammar (Can/Could/May I plus as ... as).

- **Safety note:** the topic is supportive and practical, with no graphic content: nothing about abuse, only passwords, privacy, asking a trusted adult and reporting.

## Item-bank choices

- Each Season has 65 items (9/13/7/12/10/14 across E1-E6).
- Words are A1-A2 in the open CEFR-J list (headword list, scratchpad copy). Chunks are marked A2.
- Items already used elsewhere in the roadmap were avoided (wallet, weekday and "Can I pay by card?" were swapped out).
- Pronunciation (L1 priorities for ar/es/fr/tr/it): S13 rising polite intonation (Turkish and Arabic speakers often use flat falling tone for requests) and weak "as ... as"; S14 first-syllable stress on SOMEbody/ANYthing and -s vs -ing endings.

Method: two web searches (standard mode) plus the Season research (`academy-a1s03-research.md`) and the sequencing note (`academy-lesson-sequencing.md`). Mechanics only, never content. Not retrieved: British Council, Duolingo, Busuu, Cambridge pages.

Decisions: (1) nine words are new today and five (football, gaming, drawing, music, films) return from lesson 1 inside the games, so every set of new words is small (2, 3 and 4 words) and each set is followed by two different games; (2) the verb rule is stated in three lines before the words are practised; (3) pictures are cut-out stickers made in Canva (35 % purple), objects only, no people; (4) bonus games (drag-match, shadow match, sentence match) are marked so the teacher can skip them.

# Source check of the Turkish (tr) entries in l1Watch.ts

Date: 2026-10-17. Scope: Academy hub, home language Turkish only. Source file: `src/curriculum/academy/l1Watch.ts` (118 entries whose langs include `tr`, A1-S02 to C1-S18; none in A1-S01 or Pre-A1).

## Method note

- Searches: about 25 web searches (articles, prepositions, word order, tenses, pro-drop, questions, copula, passive, modals, relative clauses, negative concord, th-substitution, /w/ /v/, /ɪ/ /iː/, s-clusters, spelling pronunciation, stress, plural after numerals, conjunctions, reported speech).
- Opened in full by curl: only two pages, <https://llexi.com/l1-errors/common-english-errors-turkish-speakers/> (a commercial blog) and <https://www.boldvoice.com/blog/english-pronunciation-turkish-speakers> (a commercial blog; its fetched text also contained Italian-speaker sections, so treat it as low reliability and I cite it for nothing except the th, r, ng, final-d topics listed under Missing).
- Everything else was seen as a search-result abstract or catalogue record only (thesis and journal landing pages). I did not read those full texts, so "supported" means "the abstract or record says so", not "I checked the data". Sample sizes are small (about 20 to 120 learners) and mostly single institutions.
- Not opened: Swan & Smith "Learner English" (not claimed), Wikipedia (not used). Statements about Turkish grammar that I give without a URL are marked "own knowledge, unsourced".
- Verdict scale: supported (a source shows this for Turkish learners or Turkish grammar), partly (the area is supported but not this exact claim or wording), contradicted (a source or Turkish grammar argues against it for tr), no source found.
- Counts (118 entries): supported 6, partly 29, contradicted 2, no source found 81.
- Most of the 81 "no source found" entries are K-marked generic errors (adds "to" after a modal, regularised verbs, "although ... but"): they are intralingual and appear for all five L1s, so a Turkish source would not exist. They can stay K; the honest label is "general learner error, not checked for tr".

## Entries to fix or drop

1. **A1-S04 "th" in thanks becomes /s/, /t/ or /f/**: drop `/f/` for tr (not found); the sourced substitute is /t/, sometimes /s/. For `ð` entries (A1-S06, A1-S09, A2-S03) the sourced substitute is /d/; /z/ not found.
2. **B1-S08 "The man who I saw him" (tr)**: doubtful. Adult Turkish relatives are pre-nominal and have no resumptive pronoun (children do use them). Remove `tr` or mark unverified. Same for **B2-S12** (resumptive pronoun in a cleft).
3. **C1-S05 modifier after the noun (tr)**: contradicted for tr. Turkish is head-final: modifiers come before the noun. Keep `tr` only for the "leaves out articles" half (split the entry).
4. **B1-S14 final /ʃ/ in wish becomes /s/ or /ʃi/ (tr)**: doubtful. Turkish has /ʃ/ (spelled ş), own knowledge, no URL opened. Remove `tr`.
5. **B1-S11 "I want that you come" (tr)**: doubtful. Turkish uses a verbal-noun clause (-mAk / -mAsInI), not a that-clause, so this is an Arabic pattern. Remove `tr` or keep K only.
6. **A2-S14 "-yor covers both"**: the FSI tables give the aorist (-r) as Turkish's habitual form and -yor as present continuous. The colloquial habitual use of -yor is real but I did not find a source. Reword to "Turkish speakers may use the present continuous for habits", keep K.
7. **A1-S09 and A1-S02 /ɪ/ /iː/ "merge"**: sources show recognition difficulty, not a proven merger, and one test used isolated vowels. Reword "often confused", keep V.
8. **A1-S02 prepositions of place** and **A1-S12, B2-S11 articles**, **A2-S01 spelled pronunciation**, **B1-S01 past simple for present perfect**: raise V-weak to V (see table).
9. **K-marked "adds to after modal / regularised verbs / double past"** entries listing `tr`: these are intralingual, not Turkish transfer (Turkish has no "to" infinitive marker). Keep K, but do not describe them as Turkish-caused in teacher notes.
10. **C1 style entries (hedging, rhetoric, irony, idioms, politeness)**: no source for Turkish, and C1-S14 already calls itself contested. Keep K with "unverified".

# Research log — Academy Deck (2026-10-10). Mechanics taken, never content; quality and limits stated honestly.

Searches were thin. What was found, and what was not:

| Source | Finding | Used for | Quality / limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Genially / Twinkl / Verbling / eTwinning escape-room write-ups (TeachersFirst review of Genially templates; Twinkl "ESL Escape Room"; eTwinning padlock activity) | Clickable objects; vocabulary tasks unlock clues; a final code (number padlock) | `padlock` (the clue is in number words), `lockers`, the "three stamps" mission frame, `findwrong` | Teacher write-ups, no efficacy data |

| Prior Academy research (docs/research/academy-game-ui.md, academy-lesson-craft-research.md) | Feedback tied to success; art serves learning; recitation helps formulaic chunks briefly | informative feedback, say-it limited to `fade` and `say` | see those files |

Not found: VIPKid / Lingokids / Gimkit slide design documentation; any controlled study of slide-deck games for A1 teens. The deck's effect on learning is **unmeasured**; the design is reasoned, not proven.

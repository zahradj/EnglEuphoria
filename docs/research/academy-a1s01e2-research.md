Design decisions from the research: (1) letters only through the student's own name; (2) spelling as tile-building with look-alike distractors; (3) number cards with dots, flags and a person holding the flag, all drawn in code; (4) error-correction as an energiser; (5) a model dialogue before every output step (the owner's rule from lesson 1).
Not found: controlled studies on tile-spelling for A1 teens; evidence here is design reasoning, not outcome data.

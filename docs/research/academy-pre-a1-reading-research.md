## What the lesson takes

1. **Activate** (Check-in): a private "I know it / New to me" sort of the lesson's words, so the 13+ student sees how much they already know. No wrong answers; the teacher sees the result.
2. **Sounds and blending** every lesson: hear the sound, blend letters into words, spell (encode) the same words.
3. **Read**: a decodable line set **and** a short message (extensive reading in miniature), read twice (listen, then read), words tappable for sound.
4. **Word bank**: the student keeps the words they want from the reading; the bank is practised the same lesson (listen and match, word tiles) and returns on the spaced schedule (Remember?, Daily 10).
5. **Reading vocabulary is the Season's vocabulary**: new items come from the texts, never from a word list the student has not read.

## Games list and video-game list (CLAUDE.md)

- Catalogue games: listen-and-match with word tiles, sound-blend slider, build-and-spell, reader page, memory pairs, the new **passage** page (read the message, keep words).
- Video-game mechanic borrowed: **loot / inventory** (the word bank: words found in the reading are collected, then spent in a game) and the **key HUD** already in the lesson.

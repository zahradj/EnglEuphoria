# Academy L1 check: Spanish (es)

Date: 2026-10-17. Scope: every entry in `src/curriculum/academy/l1Watch.ts` whose `langs` include `es` (134 entries, A1-S02 to C1-S18). No other file changed.

## Method and limits

- Extracted with a script (entry order in the file). Sources were found with web search; pages marked below as "opened" were downloaded with curl and read. Other URLs appear only as search results (title and a summary of the abstract): I did not open them, so "V" for those means "abstract-level, via search summary".
- Opened and read: Pronunciation Studio (Spanish speakers), Everest Language School (top 10 mistakes), Leonardo English (mistakes for Spanish speakers), Llexi (15 grammar mistakes), SJSU Writing Center guide "Common English-Spanish Errors" (F. de la Calle, 2018, contrastive, not a learner-data study), Wikipedia "Spanish phonology" (pointer only), the UFMG abstract on s-clusters (Portuguese and Spanish EFL speakers), the Torrado 2011 abstract on the English article.
- Not opened (search summary only): Valladolid theses, UPR dissertation, Southampton (Arche and Dominguez), Charles University thesis, UNIR gerund corpus paper, Siu thesis, elon.io and Kwiziq pages, Mango Languages.
- Swan and Smith "Learner English" was not reachable; I make no claim about it. Most tutor/blog pages are teaching sources, hence "V-weak" at best.
- The entries marked K mostly have no learner-data source in reach. "No source found" does not mean wrong; it means untested here. Where Spanish grammar itself argues against the entry I say so ("contradicted" or a fix note).
- The mark column shows current -> suggested.

## Counts

Supported 22, partly 29, contradicted 3, no source found 80 (total 134). Suggested mark changes: 40 of 134 (mostly K stays K; several V-weak to V where an abstract was seen, some V to V-weak where only teaching pages exist).

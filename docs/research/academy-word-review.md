# Word read-through: replaced B2 and C1 items and American English (2026-10-14)

After the agents replaced ~300 too-easy B2 and C1 words, every replacement was read against its Season's theme and episode role. The lists of replaced words are in the appendix (old -> new). Judgement is mine; a teacher or the owner should still skim them.

## Findings
- **Fit:** most replacements fit their Season (e.g. C1-S06 storytelling: *remorse, dismay, torment, climax, silhouette, grumble, quip*; C1-S09 register: *gig, classy, chill, tactfully*; B2-S14 change: *approximate, variation, outskirts, intersection*). Loose fits that were left: B2-S05 *pharmacist* (occupations), B2-S12 *judo, pedal, soundtrack* (student's-pick hobby skins), B2-S18 *horizons / elective / lecturer / immersion* (study-abroad texts), C1-S16 *remedial, prolific*.
- **American English (course voice):** British words found in the banks and replaced: *flat -> apartment*, *trousers -> pants*, *holiday -> vacation*, *mobile -> cell phone*, *cooker -> stove*, *bin -> garbage*, *queue -> line*, *revise -> study up*, *get good marks -> get good grades*, *a study timetable -> a study schedule*, *timetable -> class schedule*, *whilst -> henceforth*, *fresher -> newcomer*.
- **Left as they are (acceptable in American English):** *autumn*, *cinema*, *garden*, *post*, *lift (verb)*.
- **Level honesty:** some C1 picks are C1/C2 in the Octanove list (e.g. *veneration, sanctity, relinquish*); they stay because the Season is C1 and the list marks them C1+.
- **Checks:** all five levels pass `scripts/academy-check-level.ts`; no item appears twice in the roadmap.

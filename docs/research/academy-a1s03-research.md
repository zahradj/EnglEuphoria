# Research: A1 Season 3 "Free Time Club" (present simple + like + -ing), 2026-10-13

Method: four web searches (standard mode) for A1 hobby / like + -ing / "Do you like…?" lessons and common learner errors. Mechanics only are taken, never content. Not retrieved: British Council TeachingEnglish, Duolingo, Busuu and Cambridge pages (the searches returned none of them), so this note does not claim to have compared those.

| Source | What it does | Mechanic we take (and improve) | Quality / limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Twee, "My Favourite Hobby"](https://twee.com/esl-lesson-plans/my-favourite-hobby) | Online A1 lesson: match pictures to hobby words, guessing games, ask classmates | Picture-to-word matching first, then guess, then ask. We scatter the pictures (frameless) and make the guess a Teacher-vs-student game | Written for classes (asks classmates); needs 1:1 adaptation |
| [Fluentize, "Hobbies"](https://app.fluentize.com/lesson/hobbies) | A1 lesson built on a hobby music video: "I like + -ing", present simple | Song opener (catalogue #138): hear the structure in a song before it is taught | Video content not reviewed; ours would use recorded voices only (CLAUDE.md voice rule) |
| [Talkmor, A1 Hobbies](https://talkmor.com/?p=22522) and [Sports and Hobbies](https://talkmor.com/?p=23748) | 60-90 min five-part unit; tip: play for ball sports, do for individual activities, go for -ing activities | Collocation tip goes into E2 Word Lab (play football, go swimming, do yoga) | Vendor material, A1-A2 mix |
| [AmeriLingua, "What's Your Hobby?" (A1-12)](https://www.amerilingua.com/esl-lesson-plans/what-s-your-hobby) | 45 min: sort hobbies into categories, then love / like / enjoy / don't mind / hate + gerund | Category sort before the verbs; a like-scale from love to hate | Paywalled; a reviewer asked whether it is A2-B1. We keep A1 to love / like / don't like |
| Present-simple question lessons ([teach-this](https://teach-this.com/grammar/present-simple-yes-no-questions), [Busy Teacher](https://busyteacher.org/24862-present-simple-questions-and-short-answers.html), [Twee worksheet](https://twee.com/esl-grammar-worksheets/present-simple-yesno-questions-for-kids-teens)) | Contrast "I like pizza / he likes pizza", notice "do", match questions to short answers, reorder, guess-then-check about classmates, find someone who | Guided discovery (E3), question reorder, and "guess about the teacher, then check with Do you…?" (E4), which is the 1:1 form of Find-someone-who | Classroom resources; he/she/it is left to Season 5 |
| Learner-error pages ([English Plus](https://englishpluspodcast.com/like-mistakes-in-english-common-errors-and-how-to-fix-them/), [Oxford](https://teachingenglishwithoxford.oup.com/2024/02/28/help-students-with-common-grammar-mistakes/)) | "I no like", "I am enjoy", "I am liking", infinitive in lists ("hiking and to swim") | Error log categories for the teacher: missing do-support, be + like, -ing/to mix | Secondary sources; typical errors, not measured for our learners |

Decisions: (1) hobbies are taught as -ing nouns after like, with play / go / do shown as chunks in E2; (2) do-support is noticed, not explained, until E3; (3) E1 teaches no grammar, only receptive hobby words and the chunk "I like ___."; (4) the new or upgraded mechanic of the Season is the **guess-about-the-teacher-then-check** game (E4).

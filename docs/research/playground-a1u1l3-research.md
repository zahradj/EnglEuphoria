## What we took, and how we made it better

| Game (new A1 kind) | Mechanic taken | Better than the source |
|---|---|---|
| **Party Lift** (`party-lift`) | elevator floors by listening (6), wall button panel (6), teen/-ty discrimination (4) | the floor is heard, pressed, then COUNTED on the dial (counting in context); the teen pairs sit above each other on the panel so the ending decides; the last rounds make the child the caller (speaking) |
| **Dot-to-Dot Surprise** (`dot-dot`) | dot-to-dot 1-20 (7) | every dot is said aloud; picture 2 changes from digits to number WORDS (A1 reading); the reveal belongs to the birthday party |
| **Age Line-Up** (`age-lineup`) | line up by age, youngest → oldest (1, 8) | the child cannot win without asking "How old are you?" and understanding the answer; the steps make "older" visible; the finished photo becomes a speaking prompt ("Mia is six.") |
| **Party Phone** (`party-phone`) | Cambridge Starters Listening Part 2: write a spelled name and numbers (5) | a real-looking invitation instead of a worksheet line; each part can be heard again on its own; two-digit numbers typed digit by digit; the card is stamped and posted |
| Red Light, Green Light (kept, upgraded) | — | cards changed from 2-5 to 13-16 (the -teen numbers) |

Video-game mechanic borrowed: the **elevator / floor select** (Party Lift) and the **connect-the-dots reveal** (Dot-to-Dot);
the **photo mode** finish (Age Line-Up).

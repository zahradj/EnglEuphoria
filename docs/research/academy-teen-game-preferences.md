# Research — how 13-18-year-olds actually play and what they like in games, and what it means for Academy activities (2026-10-16)

Owner request (2026-10-16): "search the internet and find out how 13 to 18 year old kids actually like to play video games, and from there inspire how to create the activities." Method: 9 web searches on 2026-10-16, result summaries only, nothing read in full. Marks: [V] from a research centre or named survey, seen in a result · [V-weak] industry blog, student paper, small study · [U] not found. **Mechanics and habits only, never content.** Most numbers are about **US** teens; nothing was found for our students' home-language countries (ar, es, fr, tr, it) [U].

## 2. Honest limits
Sources are US-centric, mostly market blogs and student papers; the strong ones (Pew) give habits, not motives. Nothing here says a mechanic *teaches* English better; it says what teens already enjoy. Game ideas below are design hypotheses to pilot with real students, not proven effects.

## 3. What teens do, and what it means for Academy activities

| Pattern in the research | Design rule for an Academy activity | Where it already exists | New idea (mechanic only) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Short, repeatable rounds** (battle-arena matches, mini puzzles) | Rounds of 60-90 seconds with a clear start, finish and result; no long levels | Break the Clock, Rapport, Hot Seat, the Daily 10 | **Match Rounds**: every game is a 3-round "match" with a result card (own best, no ranking) |
| **Daily streak puzzle** (Wordle, Connections, Strands) | One small daily puzzle in the Daily 10 with a streak flame; a streak freeze so a missed day is not punished | Participle Wordle, Word Connections, Daily 10, streak flame (new) | **Daily Grid**: a Connections-style 4×4 grid from the week's words and patterns; **Strands-style** hidden word-snake on the topic |
| **Progress you can see; cosmetics as self-expression** (battle pass) | A Season track with 8 tiers (one per episode) and unlocks that are cosmetic only: banners, card backs, name plates, accent colours; no pay-to-win, no loot box, no streak shame | Season map, rank bar, Word Cards, trophy case | **Season Pass**: the 8-episode track with a tier unlocked per episode; the student picks a cosmetic at each tier |
| **Competition, but not public ranking** (leaderboards split opinion) | Compete against your own best and against the story's boss; never a public board | Own-best streaks, boss fights | **Rival Ghost**: race the "ghost" of your own last run (own-best), shown as a faded second bar |
| **Squad / friends** (72 % play multiplayer with friends) | Social play only where safe: pair tasks with the teacher, a pen-pal or a shared code; nothing with strangers | Info-gap, Alibi, Two Truths with the teacher | **Squad Quest**: a two-student co-op mission in live group lessons; async version via a code with no chat (needs the safety review) |
| **Social deduction** (Among Us, Mafia) | "Who is lying?" built on language: spot the sentence that does not fit | Alibi (new in L3), Two Truths and a Lie (E4) | **Imposter**: three statements, one is the imposter (wrong tense, wrong been/gone); the student votes and says why |
| **Battle-arena card decks** (Clash Royale, Brawl Stars) | Pick the right card from a small hand for the situation; one move, one result | Triple Match, Hot Seat | **Counter Deck**: a hand of four sentence cards; play the one that answers the opponent's line (choose-then-justify); no timer |
| **Sports and music careers** (FIFA, NBA 2K, rhythm games) | Offer topic skins so the student picks what the lesson is about (sport, music, gaming, travel, food); same language, different story | Skins ("My World"), the dial | **Career Mode** skin: press conference, transfer interview, sound check, creator Q&A, so the same pattern is practised in the student's own world |
| **Puzzle-block games** (Block Blast, Candy Crush: girls' favourites) | Fit-the-pieces puzzles; match-3 | Triple Match, Sentence Builder | **Block Fit**: chunk blocks (have + participle, never + participle) fit into a sentence grid; clear a row by completing a correct sentence |
| **Clips and sharing** (TikTok puzzle creators, streamers) | A private 15-second "highlight" of the student's best line, shareable only if they choose | Voice notes, Highlights screen | **Highlight Reel**: the best line of the lesson saved as a clip; nothing posted without the student and a guardian's choice |
| **Fun first, social second** | The first 30 seconds must be a game, not an instruction | Cinematic part cards, the Drop | Open every lesson with a micro-game round before any explanation |

Choice is a design pillar everywhere (autonomy): topic skin, cosmetic, dial, order of two tasks.

## 4. Fit with the Academy rules
Reserved and age-appropriate (no violence, no gambling feel: **no loot boxes**, no random paid rewards); no public ranking; no timers on speaking; Calm mode turns the motion off; no mascots; voices are the recorded cast. Guardian consent and a safety review before any student-to-student feature. Any new mechanic is piloted and logged in `docs/academy-video-game-mechanics.md`; mechanics only, never content.

## 5. Suggested order (for the owner's approval)
1. **Daily Grid** (Connections-style) + a daily streak with a freeze in the Daily 10: cheapest, best backed by the habit data.
2. **Season Pass** track with cosmetic tiers: uses the Season structure already built.
3. **Imposter** and **Counter Deck**: two new game kinds that reuse the Case Board code.
4. **Career Mode** skins: a content layer on existing lessons.
5. **Block Fit**, **Rival Ghost**, **Highlight Reel**: later; **Squad Quest** only after the safety review.

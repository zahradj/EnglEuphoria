# Academy structures against the English Grammar Profile (A1–B2), 2026-10-16

Source: English Grammar Profile Online (Cambridge University Press & Assessment, `englishprofile.org/?menu=egp-online`, XLSX downloaded 2026-10-16, 1,222 can-do entries from the Cambridge Learner Corpus). The file is not stored here (terms of use); levels are summarised.

**Method and limits (read first).** Each roadmap structure in `structureLevels.ts` was mapped by hand to an EGP sub-category (and, where possible, a keyword on the can-do text); the **earliest level at which any matching can-do appears** was compared with the roadmap floor. This is a coarse guide: (1) EGP shows when learners first use a form correctly with limited range, not when it should be taught for production; (2) "sub-category only" rows matched the whole sub-category, so the level is only indicative; (3) A1 learner data is sparse, so some A1 forms first appear at A2. The roadmap floor means "taught for production", so **EGP earlier = the roadmap is conservative and consistent**; **EGP later** is the only direction that would suggest a structure is placed too early.

**Result:** 48 structures mapped (A1–B2). 21 match exactly, 25 have earlier EGP evidence (conservative placement), **2 have later EGP evidence (below)**. 17 exact keyword matches are now marked `verified` in `structureLevels.ts`.

## The two "EGP later" rows, read by hand

- **imperatives (roadmap A1, EGP A2):** EGP lists the affirmative imperative, instructions, *let's*, negative imperatives at A2 and invitations at B1. No A1 imperative can-do exists in the learner data. A1 imperatives are classroom and direction chunks ("Open your book", "Turn left"); GSE and most course books place them at A1. **Keep A1, taught as chunks; do not require the negative imperative or *let's* before A2.** Source for A1: course-book practice, not EGP [K].
- **wh-questions (roadmap A1, EGP A2):** EGP lists wh- + auxiliary (do/be/have/modal) at A2; the one A1 interrogative can-do is "interrogative clauses with *be*" (What's your name? Where are you from?). **Keep A1 limited to wh- + *be* and fixed chunks; wh- + *do* ("Where do you live?") is A2 production.** **Checked in the roadmap:** A1-S01 uses wh- as chunks (fine); **A1-S10 "Right Now" has "wh-questions and question word order" at A1**, which EGP places at A2 (wh- + auxiliary *be/do*). Recommendation: keep A1-S10 to *What are you doing? / Where is he?* (wh- + *be*) as chunks with fixed word order, and move free wh- + *do* production to A2-S02 ("Travel Trouble": questions and negatives in the past simple). **Open for the owner**: change the A1-S10 structure wording, or leave it as it is.

## Other readings worth acting on
- **zero-first-conditional (B1, EGP A2 entries):** a limited use appears at A2; B1 production is fine.
- **question-tags (B1, EGP A2), defining relatives (B1, EGP A2), passive simple (B1, EGP A2), reported statements (B1, EGP A2):** all appear in learner data at A2 with limited range; B1 placement is a conservative production floor. A receptive start earlier is possible but not needed.
- **present-perfect-continuous (B2, EGP B1), perfect-modals / participle-clauses / causative (B2, EGP B1), third conditional (B2, EGP B1):** EGP has first limited uses at B1; the B2 floors are conservative. No Season moved.
- **embedded-questions (B1):** the EGP match was sub-category only; verify by hand before relying on it.

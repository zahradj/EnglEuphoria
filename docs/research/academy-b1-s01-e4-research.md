## Video-game mechanic
**Dialogue tree with an expressive guest** (visual novel / Oxenfree family) + **detective deduction** (Return of the Obra Dinn, from E3): the guest's mood face shows how the talk is going, and the host catches the false experience. Student in English: reacts, asks "When / Where / What was it like?", says which one is the lie and why.

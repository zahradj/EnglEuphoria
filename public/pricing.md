# Pricing — EnglEuphoria

All plans are monthly, billed in EUR (international) or DZD (Algeria). Every plan includes live 1-on-1 lessons with a real teacher — no group classes.

## Free Trial
- Price: €0 / 0 DZD
- Classes: 1 per month
- Includes: basic learning materials, basic support
- Not included: chat with teacher, lesson recordings

## Basic Plan
- Price: €15/month (2,500 DZD/month)
- Classes: 4 per month
- Includes: standard learning materials, standard support, chat with your teacher
- Not included: lesson recordings

## Standard Plan
- Price: €28/month (4,500 DZD/month)
- Classes: 8 per month
- Includes: premium learning materials, priority support, chat with your teacher, lesson recordings

## Premium Plan
- Price: €45/month (7,500 DZD/month)
- Classes: unlimited
- Includes: premium learning materials, priority support, chat with your teacher, lesson recordings
